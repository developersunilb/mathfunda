MathsFunda — Human Pilot Test

Stage 3 · Lesson Intelligence Pilot Study · Observer Guide

Purpose

Determine whether the Lesson Intelligence architecture actually helps a Class 9 child understand and remember mathematics better.

Your role: Observer, not teacher. Do not explain lessons during the session. Record what happens; interpret later.

  2. Circle Area — geometry-basics

  4. HCF/LCM — foundation-1-numbers-and-signs

Session Structure

Day 0 — Learn concept (~30–45 min per concept):

  1. Open MathsFunda at ./index.html
  2. Follow "Today's Learning" to the concept
  3. Read the lesson (with teaching blocks if strategy-enhanced)
  4. Complete practice questions
  5. If misconception detected → targeted intervention appears automatically
  6. Complete practice → Memory Journey opens automatically
  7. Close Memory Journey when done

Day 1 or 2 — Delayed recall (~10–15 min per concept):

  1. Without reopening the lesson, give the transfer question
  2. Ask the learner to explain their reasoning out loud
  3. Record what they remember and what they got wrong

What to Observe

  • Confusion: Where does the learner stop understanding? Hesitation? Rereading? Skipping?
  • Aha moment: When does the learner suddenly understand? "Oh, I get it." Changing from guessing to explaining.
  • Friction: What feels unnecessary or annoying? UI elements, clicking, reading load.
  • Mistakes: Let the MathsFunda flow respond. Observe: Mistake → feedback → intervention → retry → explanation.
  • Recovery: Does the child recover independently? How?
  • Memory Journey: Does the child engage with recall? Attempt answer before revealing? Skip or reveal immediately?
  • Transfer: Can they solve a new problem with the same idea?

Delayed Recall Questions (Day 1 or 2)

Do not reopen the lesson first. Do not provide hints.

ConceptTransfer Question
Linear equationsGive 3x - 4 = 11. Solve it and explain why you subtract 4 from both sides first, then divide by 3.
Circle AreaGive radius 10 cm. Find the area. Then ask: "Why did you use the radius twice in the formula?"
ExponentsGive 3⁴ × 3². Simplify it and explain what happens to the exponents and why.
HCF/LCM"Two bells ring every 14 and 21 minutes. When will they ring together?" Ask whether this is HCF or LCM and why.

Guardrails

  • Do NOT: Explain the lesson to the learner while they are using it
  • Do NOT: Optimize or change lessons after the first child
  • Do NOT: Add features, games, streaks, or rewards based on early impressions
  • Do NOT: Score everything immediately during the session
  • Do NOT: Convert observations into conclusions prematurely
  • DO: Record observations as they happen
  • DO: Let the MathsFunda flow respond to mistakes naturally
  • DO: Note unexpected behaviour (ignoring explanations, getting excited by unexpected things, using own methods)
  • DO: Share raw observations first; interpretation comes after

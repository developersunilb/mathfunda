# Foundation 2: Application Questions

Application questions for **Fractions, Decimals and Percentages**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Recipe scaling

A recipe for 6 people needs $\frac{3}{4}$ kg of flour. How much flour is needed for 8 people?

## 2. Discount

A jacket costs $\text{₹}800$ and is sold at $25\%$ off. How much is taken off the price, and what is the sale price?

## 3. Sharing a sum

$\text{₹}720$ is divided between two charities in the ratio $3:5$. How much does each charity receive?

## 4. Fuel consumption

A car uses $5.6$ litres of petrol to travel $40\text{ km}$. How many litres does it use to travel $100\text{ km}$ at the same rate?

## 5. Test score

Riya scored $42$ out of $50$ in a test. Express her score as a percentage.

## 6. Fraction of an hour

Express $45$ minutes as a fraction of an hour, in simplest form.

## 7. Decimal division

A $4.8\text{ m}$ ribbon is cut into pieces of $0.6\text{ m}$ each. How many pieces are cut?

## 8. Mixed operation

Calculate $0.375\times\frac{4}{3}+25\%\text{ of }80$.

Full worked solutions for the **Application Questions**, including diagrams where helpful.

## 1. Add complex numbers

**Question:** Simplify $(5+3i)+(2-7i)$.

**Method:** Add real and imaginary parts separately.

**Step 1 — Group.**

$$
(5+2)+(3i-7i)=7-4i
$$

$$
\boxed{7-4i}
$$

## 2. Subtract complex numbers

**Question:** Simplify $(6-4i)-(1+2i)$.

**Method:** Subtract real and imaginary parts separately. Watch the signs.

**Step 1 — Subtract.**

$$
(6-1)+(-4i-2i)=5-6i
$$

$$
\boxed{5-6i}
$$

## 3. Multiply complex numbers

**Question:** Simplify $(1+2i)(3+i)$.

**Method:** Expand and use $i^2=-1$.

**Step 1 — Expand.**

$$
1(3)+1(i)+2i(3)+2i(i)=3+i+6i+2i^2
$$

**Step 2 — Simplify.**

$$
3+7i+2(-1)=3+7i-2=1+7i
$$

$$
\boxed{1+7i}
$$

## 4. Powers of $i$

**Question:** Simplify $i^{17}$.

**Method:** Powers of $i$ repeat every 4. $17\div4=4$ remainder 1, so $i^{17}=i^1=i$.

$$
\boxed{i}
$$

## 5. Powers of $i$

**Question:** Simplify $i^{22}$.

**Method:** $22\div4=5$ remainder 2, so $i^{22}=i^2=-1$.

$$
\boxed{-1}
$$

## 6. Degrees to radians

**Question:** Convert $45^\circ$ to radians.

**Method:** Multiply by $\frac{\pi}{180}$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
45\times\frac{\pi}{180}=\frac{45\pi}{180}=\frac{\pi}{4}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{\pi}{4}\text{ radians}}
$$

## 7. Degrees to radians

**Question:** Convert $150^\circ$ to radians.

**Method:** Multiply by $\frac{\pi}{180}$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
150\times\frac{\pi}{180}=\frac{150\pi}{180}=\frac{5\pi}{6}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{5\pi}{6}\text{ radians}}
$$

## 8. Radians to degrees

**Question:** Convert $\frac{\pi}{6}$ radians to degrees.

**Method:** Multiply by $\frac{180}{\pi}$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
\frac{\pi}{6}\times\frac{180}{\pi}=\frac{180}{6}=30
$$

$$
\boxed{30^\circ}
$$

## 9. Radians to degrees

**Question:** Convert $\frac{3\pi}{2}$ radians to degrees.

**Method:** Multiply by $\frac{180}{\pi}$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
\frac{3\pi}{2}\times\frac{180}{\pi}=3\times\frac{180}{2}=3\times90=270
$$

$$
\boxed{270^\circ}
$$

## 10. Complex number modulus

**Question:** For $z=3+4i$, find $|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$.

**Method:** $|z|=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{9+16}=\sqrt{25}=5$.

$$
\boxed{5}
$$

## 11. Complex conjugate

**Question:** Write the complex conjugate of $2-5i$.

**Method:** The conjugate of $a+bi$ is $a-bi$. Change the sign of the imaginary part.

$$
\boxed{2+5i}
$$

## 12. Solve with complex numbers

**Question:** Solve $x^2+4=0$ for $x$ (allowing complex solutions).

**Method:** $x^2=-4$, so $x=\pm\sqrt{-4}=\pm\sqrt{4}\times\sqrt{-1}=\pm2i$.

**Step 1 — Isolate $x^2$.**

$$
x^2=-4
$$

**Step 2 — Take square root.**

$$
x=\pm\sqrt{-4}=\pm2i
$$

$$
\boxed{x=2i\text{ or }x=-2i}
$$

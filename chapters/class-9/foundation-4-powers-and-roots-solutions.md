# Foundation 4: Full Solutions

Full worked solutions for the **Application Questions**, including diagrams where helpful.

## 1. Area of a square

**Question:** A square tile has side $12\text{ cm}$. Find its area using index notation.

**Method:** Area of square $=\text{side}^2$.

**Step 1 — Square the side.**

$$
12^2=144
$$

$$
\boxed{12^2=144\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## 2. Volume of a cube

**Question:** A cube has edge $5\text{ cm}$. Find its volume using index notation.

**Method:** Volume of cube $=\text{edge}^3$.

**Step 1 — Cube the edge.**

$$
5^3=125
$$

$$
\boxed{5^3=125\text{ cm}^3}
$$

## 3. Bacteria growth

**Question:** A bacteria culture doubles every hour. Starting from 1 bacterium, how many after 8 hours? Write as a power of 2.

**Method:** After each hour the count multiplies by 2. After 8 hours: $1\times2^8$.

**Step 1 — Write as a power of 2.**

$$
2^8=256
$$

## 4. Paper thickness

**Question:** A sheet of paper is $0.1\text{ mm}$ thick. A stack of $10^4$ sheets — how thick is the stack in millimetres, using index notation?

**Method:** Thickness $=0.1\times10^4$ mm. Write $0.1=10^{-1}$.

**Step 1 — Combine powers.**

$$
0.1\times10^4=10^{-1}\times10^4=10^{-1+4}=10^3
$$

**Step 2 — Value.**

$$
10^3=1000\text{ mm}
$$

$$
\boxed{10^{-1}\times10^4=10^3=1000\text{ mm}}
$$

## 5. Simplify

**Question:** Simplify $\frac{2^6\times2^3}{2^4}$.

**Method:** Numerator: $2^6\times2^3=2^{6+3}=2^9$. Then divide by $2^4$: $2^{9-4}=2^5=32$.

**Step 1 — Numerator.**

$$
2^6\times2^3=2^9
$$

**Step 2 — Divide.**

$$
\frac{2^9}{2^4}=2^{9-4}=2^5=32
$$

$$
\boxed{32}
$$

## 6. Square root

**Question:** Find $\sqrt{225}$.

**Method:** Find the number squared giving 225. $15\times15=225$.

$$
\boxed{15}
$$

## 7. Cube root

**Question:** Find $\sqrt[3]{512}$.

**Method:** Test cubes. $8\times8\times8=512$.

**Step 1 — Check $8^3$.**

$$
8^3=512
$$

$$
\boxed{8}
$$

## 8. Mixed powers

**Question:** Calculate $3^3+2^4-5^2$.

**Method:** Evaluate each power, then add/subtract left to right.

**Step 1 — Powers.**

$$
3^3=27,\qquad 2^4=16,\qquad 5^2=25
$$

**Step 2 — Add and subtract.**

$$
27+16-25=43-25=18
$$

$$
\boxed{18}
$$

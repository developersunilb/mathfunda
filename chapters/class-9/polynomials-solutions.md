# Polynomials: Full Solutions

Full worked solutions for the **Application Questions**, including diagrams where helpful.

## 1. Zero of a polynomial

**Question:** Find the zero of $p(x)=4x-9$.

**Method:** Set $p(x)=0$ and solve.

**Step 1 — Set equal to zero.**

$$
4x-9=0
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
4x=9 \Rightarrow x=\frac94
$$

$$
\boxed{x=\frac94}
$$

## 2. Remainder theorem

**Question:** Find the remainder when $p(x)=2x^3+x^2-3x+5$ is divided by $x+1$.

**Method:** Remainder theorem: remainder $=p(-1)$ (since dividing by $x-(-1)$).

**Step 1 — Evaluate $p(-1)$.**

$$
p(-1)=2(-1)^3+(-1)^2-3(-1)+5=-2+1+3+5=7
$$

$$
\boxed{7}
$$

## 3. Factor theorem

**Question:** Check whether $x-1$ is a factor of $p(x)=x^3-3x^2+3x-1$.

**Method:** $x-1$ is a factor iff $p(1)=0$.

**Step 1 — Evaluate $p(1)$.**

$$
p(1)=1-3+3-1=0
$$

Since $p(1)=0$,

$$
\boxed{x-1\text{ is a factor}}
$$

## 4. Degree and type

**Question:** State the degree and type of $4x^3-2x+7$.

**Method:** Highest exponent of $x$ is 3 $\rightarrow$ degree 3 (cubic). Three terms $\rightarrow$ trinomial.

$$
\boxed{\text{Degree }3\text{ (cubic), trinomial}}
$$

## 5. Factorise

**Question:** Factorise $6x^2+11x-35$.

**Method:** Product $ac=6\times(-35)=-210$, sum $11$: numbers $21$ and $-10$.

**Step 1 — Split.**

$$
6x^2+21x-10x-35
$$

**Step 2 — Group.**

$$
3x(2x+7)-5(2x+7)
$$

**Step 3 — Common bracket.**

$$
(2x+7)(3x-5)
$$

$$
\boxed{(2x+7)(3x-5)}
$$

Check: $(2x+7)(3x-5)=6x^2-10x+21x-35=6x^2+11x-35$. ✓

## 6. Zero of a quadratic

**Question:** Find the zeroes of $p(x)=x^2-7x+12$.

**Method:** Factorise: numbers with product 12 and sum -7 are $-3,-4$.

**Step 1 — Factorise.**

$$
x^2-7x+12=(x-3)(x-4)
$$

**Step 2 — Set each factor to zero.**

$$
x-3=0 \Rightarrow x=3,\qquad x-4=0 \Rightarrow x=4
$$

$$
\boxed{x=3\text{ or }x=4}
$$

## 7. Identity — expand

**Question:** Expand $(x+3)(x-2)$.

**Method:** FOIL — multiply each term in the first bracket by each in the second.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
x^2-2x+3x-6
$$

**Step 2 — Combine.**

$$
x^2+x-6
$$

$$
\boxed{x^2+x-6}
$$

## 8. Identity — difference of squares

**Question:** Factorise $9x^2-16$.

**Method:** Recognise $(3x)^2-4^2=(3x-4)(3x+4)$.

$$
9x^2-16=(3x)^2-4^2=(3x-4)(3x+4)
$$

$$
\boxed{(3x-4)(3x+4)}
$$

## 9. Remainder theorem

**Question:** Find the remainder when $p(x)=x^3-4x^2+x+6$ is divided by $x-3$.

**Method:** Remainder $=p(3)$.

**Step 1 — Evaluate $p(3)$.**

$$
p(3)=3^3-4(3)^2+3+6=27-36+3+6=0
$$

$$
\boxed{0}
$$

Since the remainder is 0, $x-3$ is also a factor.

## 10. Factorise completely

**Question:** Factorise $x^2-x-12$.

**Method:** Product -12, sum -1: numbers $-4$ and $+3$.

**Step 1 — Split and group.**

$$
x^2-x-12=x^2-4x+3x-12=x(x-4)+3(x-4)
$$

**Step 2 — Common bracket.**

$$
(x-4)(x+3)
$$

$$
\boxed{(x-4)(x+3)}
$$

## 11. Algebraic identity

**Question:** Expand $(2x+5)^2$.

**Method:** $(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2$ with $a=2x$, $b=5$.

**Step 1 — Apply.**

$$
(2x)^2+2(2x)(5)+5^2=4x^2+20x+25
$$

$$
\boxed{4x^2+20x+25}
$$

## 12. Verify zero

**Question:** Verify that $x=2$ is a zero of $p(x)=x^3-6x^2+11x-6$.

**Method:** Evaluate $p(2)$; if it is 0, then $x=2$ is a zero.

**Step 1 — Evaluate $p(2)$.**

$$
p(2)=2^3-6(2)^2+11(2)-6=8-24+22-6=0
$$

Since $p(2)=0$,

$$
\boxed{x=2\text{ is a zero of }p(x)}
$$

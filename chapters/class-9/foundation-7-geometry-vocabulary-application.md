# Foundation 7: Application Questions

Application questions for **Geometry Vocabulary**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Missing angle

Two angles on a straight line are $x$ and $125^\circ$. Find $x$.

## 2. Triangle third angle

Two angles of a triangle are $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. Find the third angle.

## 3. Classify triangle

A triangle has side lengths 7 cm, 7 cm, 7 cm. Classify it.

## 4. Classify triangle

A triangle has side lengths 6 cm, 6 cm, 10 cm. Classify it.

## 5. Quadrant

In which quadrant does $(-4,-5)$ lie?

## 6. Quadrant

In which quadrant does $(3,-2)$ lie?

## 7. Midpoint

Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(2,4)$ and $(8,10)$.

## 8. Midpoint

Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(-2,4)$ and $(4,-2)$.

## 9. Right angle check

A triangle has angles $35^\circ, 55^\circ$ and $90^\circ$. What kind of triangle is it by its angles?

## 10. Angle sum of quadrilateral

Three angles of a quadrilateral are $85^\circ, 95^\circ$ and $110^\circ$. Find the fourth angle.

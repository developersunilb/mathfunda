# Geometry Basics: Application Questions

Application questions for **Perimeter, Area and Heron's Formula**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Perimeter of a rectangle

Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

## 2. Area of a rectangle

Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

## 3. Area of a triangle

Find the area of a triangle with base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.

## 4. Heron's formula

Find the area of a triangle with sides $5\text{ cm}, 6\text{ cm}, 7\text{ cm}$.

## 5. Circumference

Find the circumference of a circle of radius $7\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$).

## 6. Fencing a garden

A rectangular garden measures $15\text{ m}$ by $9\text{ m}$. Find the fencing needed (perimeter).

## 7. Tiling a floor

A rectangular floor measures $4\text{ m}$ by $3.5\text{ m}$. Tiles of side $0.5\text{ m}$. How many tiles?

## 8. Triangle banner

A triangular banner has base $2.4\text{ m}$ and height $1.5\text{ m}$. Find its area.

## 9. Heron's formula

Find the area of a triangle with sides $6\text{ cm}, 8\text{ cm}, 10\text{ cm}$.

## 10. Circle area

Find the area of a circle of radius $14\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$).

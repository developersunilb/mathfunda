# Polynomials: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Polynomials**: degree, zeroes, remainder and factor theorems, identities, factorisation.

## Example 1: Degree and type

**Question:** State the degree and type of $4x^3-2x+7$.

**Method:** The degree is the highest exponent of $x$, which is 3. Three terms $\rightarrow$ trinomial.

$$
\boxed{\text{Degree }3,\text{ trinomial (cubic)}}
$$

## Example 2: Zero of a linear polynomial

**Question:** Find the zero of $p(x)=4x-9$.

**Method:** Set $p(x)=0$ and solve.

**Step 1 — Set equal to zero.**

$$
4x-9=0
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
4x=9 \Rightarrow x=\frac94
$$

$$
\boxed{x=\frac94}
$$

## Example 3: Remainder theorem

**Question:** Find the remainder when $p(x)=x^3-2x^2+4$ is divided by $x-2$.

**Method:** Remainder theorem: remainder $=p(2)$.

**Step 1 — Evaluate $p(2)$.**

$$
p(2)=2^3-2(2)^2+4=8-8+4=4
$$

$$
\boxed{4}
$$

## Example 4: Factor theorem

**Question:** Check whether $x-1$ is a factor of $p(x)=x^3-3x^2+3x-1$.

**Method:** $x-1$ is a factor iff $p(1)=0$.

**Step 1 — Evaluate $p(1)$.**

$$
p(1)=1-3+3-1=0
$$

Since $p(1)=0$, $\boxed{x-1\text{ is a factor}}$.

## Example 5: Factorise by splitting the middle term

**Question:** Factorise $6x^2+11x-35$.

**Method:** Find two numbers with product $ac=6\times(-35)=-210$ and sum $b=11$: they are $21$ and $-10$.

**Step 1 — Split the middle term.**

$$
6x^2+21x-10x-35
$$

**Step 2 — Group in pairs.**

$$
(6x^2+21x)+(-10x-35)=3x(2x+7)-5(2x+7)
$$

**Step 3 — Take out the common bracket.**

$$
(2x+7)(3x-5)
$$

$$
\boxed{(2x+7)(3x-5)}
$$

Check by expanding: $(2x+7)(3x-5)=6x^2-10x+21x-35=6x^2+11x-35$. ✓

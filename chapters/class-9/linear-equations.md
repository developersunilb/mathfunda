## 1. Linear Equations in Two Variables

A linear equation in two variables $x$ and $y$ can be written in the standard form

$$
ax+by+c=0
$$

where $a$, $b$ and $c$ are real numbers, and at least one of $a$ or $b$ is not zero.

Examples:

- $2x+3y=12$
- $x-4y=0$
- $y=5$

> **Remember:** A linear equation in two variables has infinitely many solutions.

## 2. Solutions of a Linear Equation

A solution is an ordered pair $(x,y)$ that makes the equation true.

**Example:** For $2x+3y=12$:

| $x$ | $y$ | Check |
|---|---|---|
| $0$ | $4$ | $2(0)+3(4)=12$ |
| $3$ | $2$ | $2(3)+3(2)=12$ |
| $6$ | $0$ | $2(6)+3(0)=12$ |

Each pair can be written as $(0,4)$, $(3,2)$ and $(6,0)$.

### Finding a solution

**Problem:** Find two solutions of $x+2y=8$.

Choose $x=0$:

$$
0+2y=8 \quad\Rightarrow\quad y=4
$$

Choose $x=4$:

$$
4+2y=8 \quad\Rightarrow\quad 2y=4 \quad\Rightarrow\quad y=2
$$

Two solutions are $(0,4)$ and $(4,2)$.

## 3. Graph of a Linear Equation

The graph of a linear equation in two variables is a straight line.

To draw it:

1. Find two solutions.
2. Plot the points on the coordinate plane.
3. Join the points with a straight line.
4. Extend the line in both directions.

### Example: graph of $x+y=4$

| $x$ | $0$ | $4$ |
|---|---|---|
| $y$ | $4$ | $0$ |

The line passes through $(0,4)$ and $(4,0)$.

## 4. Lines Parallel to the Axes

- $x=a$ is a vertical line parallel to the $y$-axis.
- $y=b$ is a horizontal line parallel to the $x$-axis.

For example, $x=3$ is a vertical line through $(3,0)$, and $y=-2$ is a horizontal line through $(0,-2)$.

## 5. Word Problems

### Example: age problem

**Problem:** The sum of the ages of a father and his son is $50$ years. Write a linear equation for the situation.

Let the father's age be $x$ years and the son's age be $y$ years:

$$
x+y=50
$$

If the son is $14$ years old, then:

$$
x+14=50 \quad\Rightarrow\quad x=36
$$

The father is $36$ years old.

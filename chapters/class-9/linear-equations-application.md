# Linear Equations: Application Questions

Application questions for **Linear Equations in Two Variables**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Table of values

Complete a table of values for $y=3x-2$ at $x=0,1,2,3,4$.

## 2. Graph and gradient

Draw the graph of $y=x+2$ and state its gradient and y-intercept.

## 3. Solve

Solve $2x-5=11$.

## 4. Solve

Solve $\frac{x}{4}=9$.

## 5. Equation from two points

Find the equation of the line through $(0,4)$ and $(2,8)$.

## 6. Check a solution

Is $(3,10)$ a solution of $y=3x+1$?

## 7. Real-life linear relationship

A taxi charges a ₹50 flag-fall plus ₹12 per km. Write an equation for the cost $C$ for $d$ km, and find the cost for 15 km.

## 8. Graph interpretation

The graph of a line passes through $(0,3)$ and $(4,11)$. Find its gradient and write its equation.

## 9. Solve

Solve $5x+3=3x+11$.

## 10. Intersection

Two lines are $y=2x+1$ and $y=x+4$. Find the point where they intersect.

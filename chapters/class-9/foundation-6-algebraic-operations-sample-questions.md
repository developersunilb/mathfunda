# Foundation 6: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Expand $(x+3)(x-2)$.
2. Expand $(a+b)(a-b)$.
3. Factorise $x^2-7x+12$.
4. Expand $(2x+5)^2$.
5. Factorise $9x^2-16$.
6. A rectangle has length $(x+5)$ m and width $(x-2)$ m. Write and simplify an expression for its area.
7. A square garden has side $(2x+3)$ m. Write and simplify an expression for its area.
8. Factorise $x^2+5x+6$.
9. Factorise $16y^2-25$.
10. Simplify $(3a-4)(3a+4)$.
11. Expand and simplify $(x-7)(x+2)$.
12. Expand $(5t-3)^2$.
13. Factorise $x^2-x-12$.
14. Expand $(x+4)(x-6)$.
15. Factorise $x^2+7x+12$.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

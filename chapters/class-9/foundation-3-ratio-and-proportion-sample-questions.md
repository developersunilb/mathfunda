# Foundation 3: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Divide £60 in the ratio 2:3.
2. 300 ml of red paint is mixed with blue paint in ratio 3:1. How much blue paint is needed?
3. Simplify the ratio 18:24.
4. 5 pens cost ₹75. Find the cost of 8 pens.
5. A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. How far in 7 hours at the same speed?
6. Pink paint is made by mixing red and white in the ratio 2:5. How much red and white paint are needed to make 350 ml?
7. ₹900 is shared between A, B and C in the ratio 2:3:4. How much does each receive?
8. A cookie recipe uses flour and butter in the ratio 4:1. If 600 g flour is used, how much butter?
9. A map scale is 1:50,000. Two towns are 6 cm apart on the map. Find the actual distance in km.
10. 8 notebooks cost ₹240. Find the cost of 15 notebooks.
11. A cyclist covers 36 km in 3 hours. How long to cover 60 km at the same speed?
12. The ratio of ages of a father and son is 7:3. If the son is 15, how old is the father?
13. A class has 18 boys and 12 girls. Write the ratio of boys to girls in simplest form.
14. £150 is divided in the ratio 3:2. Find each share.
15. 4 workers can build a wall in 6 days. How many days for 6 workers (same rate)?

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

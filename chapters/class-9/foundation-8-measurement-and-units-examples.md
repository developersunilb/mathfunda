# Foundation 8: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Measurement and Units**: unit conversion, perimeter and area formulas.

## Example 1: Unit conversion

**Question:** Convert $3.5\text{ km}$ to metres.

**Method:** $1\text{ km}=1000\text{ m}$. Multiply by 1000.

$$
3.5\times1000=3500
$$

$$
\boxed{3500\text{ m}}
$$

## Example 2: Perimeter of a rectangle

**Question:** Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Perimeter $=2(l+w)$.

**Step 1 — Add length and width.**

$$
12.5+8=20.5
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply by 2.**

$$
2\times20.5=41
$$

$$
\boxed{41\text{ cm}}
$$

## Example 3: Area of a rectangle

**Question:** Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Area $=\text{length}\times\text{width}$.

$$
12.5\times8=100
$$

$$
\boxed{100\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## Example 4: Area of a triangle

**Question:** Find the area of a triangle with base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Area $=\frac12\times\text{base}\times\text{height}$.

$$
\frac12\times10\times6=30
$$

$$
\boxed{30\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## Example 5: Unit conversion — cm to m

**Question:** Convert $245\text{ cm}$ to metres.

**Method:** $100\text{ cm}=1\text{ m}$. Divide by 100.

$$
245\div100=2.45
$$

$$
\boxed{2.45\text{ m}}
$$

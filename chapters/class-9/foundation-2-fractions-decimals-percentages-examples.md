# Foundation 2: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Fractions, Decimals and Percentages**.

## Example 1: Add fractions with different denominators

**Question:** Find $\frac{2}{3}+\frac{3}{4}$.

**Step 1 — Find a common denominator.** LCM of $3$ and $4$ is $12$.

**Step 2 — Convert each fraction.**

$$
\frac{2}{3}=\frac{8}{12},\qquad \frac{3}{4}=\frac{9}{12}
$$

**Step 3 — Add.**

$$
\frac{8}{12}+\frac{9}{12}=\frac{17}{12}=1\frac{5}{12}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{17}{12}=1\frac{5}{12}}
$$

## Example 2: Multiply decimals

**Question:** Calculate $3.75\times0.4$.

**Method:** Multiply as whole numbers, then place the decimal point using the total number of decimal places.

**Step 1 — Multiply as whole numbers.**

$$
375\times4=1500
$$

**Step 2 — Count decimal places.** $3.75$ has $2$, $0.4$ has $1$, so $3$ places total.

$$
1500\rightarrow1.500=1.5
$$

$$
\boxed{1.5}
$$

## Example 3: Percentage of a quantity

**Question:** Find $15\%$ of $240$.

**Method:** Write $15\%$ as a fraction or decimal and multiply.

$$
15\%=\frac{15}{100}=0.15,\qquad 0.15\times240=36
$$

$$
\boxed{36}
$$

## Example 4: Divide fractions

**Question:** Find $\frac{5}{6}\div\frac{2}{3}$.

**Method:** Dividing by a fraction is multiplying by its reciprocal.

**Step 1 — Reciprocal of $\frac{2}{3}$ is $\frac{3}{2}$.**

**Step 2 — Multiply.**

$$
\frac{5}{6}\times\frac{3}{2}=\frac{15}{12}=\frac{5}{4}=1\frac{1}{4}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{5}{4}=1\frac{1}{4}}
$$

## Example 5: Write a decimal as a fraction

**Question:** Write $0.375$ as a fraction in simplest form.

**Method:** Write over a power of 10, then cancel the highest common factor.

**Step 1 — Write over $1000$.**

$$
0.375=\frac{375}{1000}
$$

**Step 2 — Cancel by HCF $125$.**

$$
\frac{375\div125}{1000\div125}=\frac{3}{8}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{3}{8}}
$$

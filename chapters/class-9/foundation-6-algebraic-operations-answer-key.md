# Foundation 6: Answer Key (Parent Only)

This page contains the answers and full solution methods for the **Practice Questions**. Keep it away from the student until they have attempted the questions.

> **How to use:** Ask your child to show their working for each question. Mark correct with a tick. For any wrong answer, go back to the first step where the method slipped and explain it using the method below — do not just give the final answer.

## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** FOIL: $x^2-2x+3x-6=x^2+x-6$.

**Q2.** $(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-ab+ab-b^2=\boxed{a^2-b^2}$.

**Q3.** Product 12, sum -7: numbers $-3,-4$. $\boxed{(x-3)(x-4)}$.

**Q4.** $(2x+5)^2=4x^2+20x+25$.

**Q5.** $(3x)^2-4^2=\boxed{(3x-4)(3x+4)}$.

**Q6.** $(x+5)(x-2)=x^2-2x+5x-10=\boxed{x^2+3x-10\text{ m}^2}$.

**Q7.** $(2x+3)^2=4x^2+12x+9$.

**Q8.** Product 6, sum 5: numbers $2,3$. $\boxed{(x+2)(x+3)}$.

**Q9.** $(4y)^2-5^2=\boxed{(4y-5)(4y+5)}$.

**Q10.** $(3a-4)(3a+4)=(3a)^2-4^2=\boxed{9a^2-16}$.

**Q11.** $(x-7)(x+2)=x^2+2x-7x-14=\boxed{x^2-5x-14}$.

**Q12.** $(5t-3)^2=25t^2-30t+9$.

**Q13.** Product -12, sum -1: numbers $-4,+3$. Group: $x^2-4x+3x-12=x(x-4)+3(x-4)=\boxed{(x-4)(x+3)}$.

**Q14.** $(x+4)(x-6)=x^2-6x+4x-24=\boxed{x^2-2x-24}$.

**Q15.** Product 12, sum 7: numbers $3,4$. $\boxed{(x+3)(x+4)}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) writing $(a+b)^2=a^2+b^2$ (forgetting the $2ab$ term); (ii) sign errors when expanding $(x-7)(x+2)$; (iii) choosing factors with the wrong sum; (iv) forgetting the middle term cancels in difference of squares. Revisit Foundation 6 if several of these appear.

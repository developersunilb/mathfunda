# Foundation 1: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Simplify $-12+5$.
2. Simplify $7-15$.
3. Simplify $(-6)\times(-7)$.
4. Simplify $(-45)\div(+9)$.
5. Simplify $18-4\times3$.
6. Simplify $5+(12\div3)^2$.
7. Simplify $2\times(7+3)-8\div4$.
8. Simplify $12\div3\times2$.
9. Find $\lvert-11\rvert+\lvert3\rvert$.
10. In $8,24,305$, state the place value of $2$ and the face value of $8$.
11. Which is greater, $-12$ or $-20$?
12. A number leaves remainder $3$ when divided by $7$. Write it in the form dividend $=$ divisor $\times$ quotient $+$ remainder, using quotient $5$.
13. At 6 a.m. the temperature was $-4^\circ\text{C}$. By noon it rose by $9^\circ\text{C}$, then by evening it fell by $7^\circ\text{C}$. Find the evening temperature.
14. A shopkeeper buys an item for $\text{₹}120$ and sells it for $\text{₹}95$. Find the signed change from the cost price and the loss.
15. A point on a hill is $84\text{ m}$ above sea level and a point in a valley is $37\text{ m}$ below sea level. Find the difference in height between them.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

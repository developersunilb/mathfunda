# Foundation 7: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Geometry Vocabulary**: angles, triangles, quadrilaterals and coordinates.

## Example 1: Angles on a straight line

**Question:** Two angles on a straight line are $x$ and $125^\circ$. Find $x$.

**Method:** Angles on a straight line add to $180^\circ$.

$$
x+125=180
$$

$$
x=55
$$

$$
\boxed{x=55^\circ}
$$

</pre>
<p>$x+125=180 \Rightarrow x=55^\circ$.</p>

## Example 2: Angles in a triangle

**Question:** Two angles of a triangle are $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. Find the third angle.

**Method:** Angles in a triangle add to $180^\circ$.

$$
180-48-67=65
$$

$$
\boxed{65^\circ}
$$

## Example 3: Types of triangle

**Question:** Classify a triangle with side lengths 5 cm, 5 cm, 8 cm.

**Method:** Two sides are equal, so it is an **isosceles** triangle.

$$
\boxed{\text{Isosceles triangle}}
$$

## Example 4: Coordinates and quadrant

**Question:** In which quadrant does $(-3,4)$ lie?

**Method:** $x$ is negative, $y$ is positive $\rightarrow$ Quadrant II.

$$
\boxed{\text{Quadrant II}}
$$

<p>$(-3,4)$: $x<0$, $y>0$ $\rightarrow$ Quadrant II.</p>

## Example 5: Midpoint

**Question:** Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(2,4)$ and $(8,10)$.

**Method:** Midpoint $=\left(\frac{x_1+x_2}{2},\frac{y_1+y_2}{2}\right)$.

**Step 1 — Average the coordinates.**

$$
\left(\frac{2+8}{2},\frac{4+10}{2}\right)=\left(\frac{10}{2},\frac{14}{2}\right)=(5,7)
$$

$$
\boxed{(5,7)}
$$

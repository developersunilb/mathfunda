# Beyond Class 9: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Beyond Class 9**: complex numbers and radians.

## Example 1: Complex numbers — add and subtract

**Question:** Simplify $(3+2i)+(4-5i)$.

**Method:** Add real parts and imaginary parts separately.

**Step 1 — Group.**

$$
(3+4)+(2i-5i)=7-3i
$$

$$
\boxed{7-3i}
$$

## Example 2: Complex numbers — multiply

**Question:** Simplify $(2+3i)(1-4i)$.

**Method:** Expand like brackets, remembering $i^2=-1$.

**Step 1 — Expand.**

$$
2(1)+2(-4i)+3i(1)+3i(-4i)=2-8i+3i-12i^2
$$

**Step 2 — Use $i^2=-1$.**

$$
2-5i-12(-1)=2-5i+12=14-5i
$$

$$
\boxed{14-5i}
$$

## Example 3: Complex numbers — powers of $i$

**Question:** Simplify $i^{23}$.

**Method:** Powers of $i$ repeat in a cycle of 4: $i^1=i$, $i^2=-1$, $i^3=-i$, $i^4=1$. Divide the exponent by 4 and use the remainder.

**Step 1 — Remainder.**

$$
23\div4=5\text{ remainder }3
$$

**Step 2 — Use remainder.**

$$
i^{23}=i^3=-i
$$

$$
\boxed{-i}
$$

## Example 4: Radians — convert degrees to radians

**Question:** Convert $60^\circ$ to radians.

**Method:** Multiply by $\frac{\pi}{180}$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
60\times\frac{\pi}{180}=\frac{60\pi}{180}=\frac{\pi}{3}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{\pi}{3}\text{ radians}}
$$

## Example 5: Radians — convert radians to degrees

**Question:** Convert $\frac{\pi}{4}$ radians to degrees.

**Method:** Multiply by $\frac{180}{\pi}$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
\frac{\pi}{4}\times\frac{180}{\pi}=\frac{180}{4}=45
$$

$$
\boxed{45^\circ}
$$

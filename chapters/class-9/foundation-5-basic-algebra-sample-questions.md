# Foundation 5: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Simplify $3x+5x-2x$.
2. Expand $4(2x-3)+5$.
3. Solve $3x+7=22$.
4. Solve $\frac{x}{4}=9$.
5. Expand and simplify $2(x-5)+3(x+1)$.
6. A rectangle has length $(x+4)$ cm and width $x$ cm. Write and simplify an expression for its perimeter.
7. A notebook costs ₹$x$ and a pen costs ₹5 less. Write and simplify an expression for 3 notebooks and 2 pens.
8. A number is doubled and 7 is added, giving 25. Find the number.
9. Sarah is $x$ years old; her brother is 3 years older. Combined age is 27. Find Sarah's age.
10. A rectangular garden has width $w$ and length $w+3$ m. Perimeter is 30 m. Find $w$.
11. Pens cost ₹$p$ each. 6 pens cost ₹72. Find $p$.
12. Triangle angles are $x$, $x+20$, and $90$. Find $x$.
13. Simplify $5(2a-3)-2(a+4)$.
14. Simplify $7m-3n+2m-5n$.
15. Solve $x-8=15$.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

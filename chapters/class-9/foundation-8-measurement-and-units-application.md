# Foundation 8: Application Questions

Application questions for **Measurement and Units**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Unit conversion

Convert $5.2\text{ km}$ to metres.

## 2. Perimeter

Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

## 3. Area

Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

## 4. Triangle area

Find the area of a triangle with base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.

## 5. Unit conversion

Convert $245\text{ cm}$ to metres.

## 6. Fencing a garden

A rectangular garden measures $15\text{ m}$ by $9\text{ m}$. Find the length of fencing needed to go around it (the perimeter).

## 7. Tiling a floor

A rectangular floor measures $4\text{ m}$ by $3.5\text{ m}$. Square tiles of side $0.5\text{ m}$ are used. How many tiles are needed?

## 8. Triangle banner

A triangular banner has base $2.4\text{ m}$ and height $1.5\text{ m}$. Find its area.

## 9. Unit conversion — mm to cm

Convert $85\text{ mm}$ to centimetres.

## 10. Paint for a wall

A rectangular wall is $6\text{ m}$ wide and $2.5\text{ m}$ high. One litre of paint covers $8\text{ m}^2$. How many litres are needed?

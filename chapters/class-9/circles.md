# Chapter 5: Circles

## 5.1 Circle vocabulary

A circle is the set of all points equidistant from a fixed point (centre). Radius $r$, diameter $d=2r$, circumference $C=2\pi r$, area $A=\pi r^2$.

## 5.2 Chords and arcs

A chord is a line segment with both endpoints on the circle. The longest chord is the diameter.

## 5.3 Angle at centre

The angle subtended by an arc at the centre is twice the angle subtended at any point on the remaining circumference.

## 5.4 Cyclic quadrilateral

A quadrilateral inscribed in a circle has opposite angles summing to $180^\circ$.

## 5.5 Tangents

A tangent to a circle is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact.

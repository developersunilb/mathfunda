# Foundation 2: Answer Key (Parent Only)

This page contains the answers and full solution methods for the **Practice Questions**. Keep it away from the student until they have attempted the questions.

> **How to use:** Ask your child to show their working for each question. Mark correct with a tick. For any wrong answer, go back to the first step where the method slipped and explain it using the method below — do not just give the final answer.

## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** LCM of $3,4$ is $12$: $\frac{2}{3}=\frac8{12}$, $\frac{3}{4}=\frac9{12}$, sum $\frac{17}{12}=1\frac5{12}$.

**Q2.** $375\times4=1500$; $2+1=3$ decimal places $\rightarrow \boxed{1.5}$.

**Q3.** $15\%=\frac{15}{100}=0.15$; $0.15\times240=\boxed{36}$.

**Q4.** Reciprocal of $\frac23$ is $\frac32$: $\frac56\times\frac32=\frac{15}{12}=\frac54=1\frac14$.

**Q5.** $0.375=\frac{375}{1000}=\frac38$ (divide by $125$).

**Q6.** LCM of $8,6$ is $24$: $\frac58=\frac{15}{24}$, $\frac16=\frac4{24}$, sum $\frac{19}{24}$.

**Q7.** $0.6\times0.25=\frac6{10}\times\frac14=\frac6{40}=\frac3{20}=\boxed{0.15}$.

**Q8.** $40\%=\frac{40}{100}=0.4$; $0.4\times350=\boxed{140}$.

**Q9.** $0.6=\frac6{10}=\boxed{\frac35}$.

**Q10.** $\frac35\div\frac27=\frac35\times\frac72=\frac{21}{10}=2\frac1{10}$.

**Q11.** Per person: $\frac23\div4=\frac{1}{6}$ kg. For 6: $\frac16\times6=\boxed{1\text{ kg}}$.

**Q12.** Discount: $15\%\times600=0.15\times600=\boxed{\text{₹}90}$. Sale: $600-90=\boxed{\text{₹}510}$.

**Q13.** Total parts $5$; one part $900\div5=180$. Shares $2\times180=\boxed{\text{₹}360}$, $3\times180=\boxed{\text{₹}540}$.

**Q14.** $\frac{30}{60}=\boxed{\frac12}$.

**Q15.** $\frac38\times\frac43=\frac12$; $25\%\times80=20$; sum $\boxed{20.5}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) adding denominators when adding fractions; (ii) misplacing the decimal point in decimal multiplication; (iii) forgetting to simplify fractions; (iv) working percentage "of" as addition instead of multiplication. Revisit Foundation 2 if several of these appear.

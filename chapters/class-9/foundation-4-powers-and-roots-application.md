# Foundation 4: Application Questions

Application questions for **Powers and Roots**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Area of a square

A square tile has side $12\text{ cm}$. Find its area using index notation.

## 2. Volume of a cube

A cube has edge $5\text{ cm}$. Find its volume using index notation.

## 3. Bacteria growth

A bacteria culture doubles every hour. Starting from 1 bacterium, how many are there after 8 hours? Write as a power of 2.

## 4. Paper thickness

A sheet of paper is $0.1\text{ mm}$ thick. A stack of $10^4$ sheets — how thick is the stack in millimetres, using index notation?

## 5. Simplify

Simplify $\frac{2^6\times2^3}{2^4}$.

## 6. Square root

Find $\sqrt{225}$.

## 7. Cube root

Find $\sqrt[3]{512}$.

## 8. Mixed powers

Calculate $3^3+2^4-5^2$.

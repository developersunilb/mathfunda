# Basic Equations: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Solve $3x+7=22$.
2. Solve $\frac{x}{4}=9$.
3. Solve $2x-5=11$.
4. Solve $4(x-3)=20$.
5. Solve $5x+3=3x+11$.
6. A number is doubled and 7 is added, giving 25. Find the number.
7. Pens cost ₹$p$ each. 6 pens cost ₹72. Find $p$.
8. Sarah is $x$ years old; her brother is 3 years older. Combined age is 27. Find Sarah's age.
9. A rectangular garden has width $w$ m and length $w+3$ m. Perimeter is 30 m. Find $w$.
10. Two angles of a triangle are $x$ and $x+20$, and the third is $90^\circ$. Find $x$.
11. A mobile plan costs ₹$m$ per month plus ₹150 activation fee. First month total ₹850. Find $m$.
12. A box of sweets shared among 5 children gives 8 each. How many sweets in the box?
13. Solve $7x-4=31$.
14. Solve $\frac{x+2}{3}=5$.
15. Solve $3(x+4)=27$.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

## 5. Equation from two points

**Question:** Find the equation of the line through $(0,4)$ and $(2,8)$.

**Method:** Gradient $m=\frac{8-4}{2-0}=\frac42=2$. Since $(0,4)$ is the y-intercept, $c=4$.

**Step 1 — Gradient.**

$$
m=\frac{8-4}{2-0}=2
$$

**Step 2 — Equation.**

$$
\boxed{y=2x+4}
$$

## 6. Check a solution

**Question:** Is $(3,10)$ a solution of $y=3x+1$?

**Method:** Substitute $x=3$: $y=3(3)+1=9+1=10$, which matches $y=10$.

$$
\boxed{\text{Yes}}
$$

## 7. Real-life linear relationship

**Question:** A taxi charges a ₹50 flag-fall plus ₹12 per km. Write an equation for the cost $C$ for $d$ km, and find the cost for 15 km.

**Method:** Cost $C=50+12d$. For $d=15$: $C=50+12(15)=50+180=230$.

**Step 1 — Equation.**

$$
C=50+12d
$$

**Step 2 — Cost for 15 km.**

$$
C=50+12(15)=50+180=230
$$

$$
\boxed{C=50+12d;\quad \text{cost for 15 km }=₹230}
$$

## 8. Graph interpretation

**Question:** The graph of a line passes through $(0,3)$ and $(4,11)$. Find its gradient and write its equation.

**Method:** Gradient $m=\frac{11-3}{4-0}=\frac84=2$. y-intercept $c=3$ (from $(0,3)$).

**Step 1 — Gradient.**

$$
m=\frac{11-3}{4-0}=2
$$

**Step 2 — Equation.**

$$
\boxed{y=2x+3}
$$

2x=8

## 10. Intersection

**Question:** Two lines are $y=2x+1$ and $y=x+4$. Find the point where they intersect.

**Method:** At the intersection, the $y$ values are equal: $2x+1=x+4$.

**Step 1 — Solve for $x$.**

$$
2x+1=x+4
$$

$$
x=3
$$

**Step 2 — Find $y$.**

$$
y=2(3)+1=7
$$

$$
\boxed{(3,7)}
$$

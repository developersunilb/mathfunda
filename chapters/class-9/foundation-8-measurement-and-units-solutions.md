## 1. Unit conversion

**Question:** Convert $5.2\text{ km}$ to metres.

**Method:** $1\text{ km}=1000\text{ m}$. Multiply by 1000.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
5.2\times1000=5200
$$

$$
\boxed{5200\text{ m}}
$$

## 2. Perimeter

**Question:** Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Perimeter $=2(l+w)$.

**Step 1 — Add.**

$$
12.5+8=20.5
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply by 2.**

$$
2\times20.5=41
$$

$$
\boxed{41\text{ cm}}
$$

## 3. Area

**Question:** Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Area $=l\times w$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
12.5\times8=100
$$

$$
\boxed{100\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## 4. Triangle area

**Question:** Find the area of a triangle with base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Area $=\frac12\times b\times h$.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
\frac12\times10\times6=5\times6=30
$$

$$
\boxed{30\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## 6. Fencing a garden

**Question:** A rectangular garden measures $15\text{ m}$ by $9\text{ m}$. Find the length of fencing needed (perimeter).

**Method:** Perimeter $=2(l+w)=2(15+9)$.

**Step 1 — Add.**

$$
15+9=24
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply by 2.**

$$
2\times24=48
$$

$$
\boxed{48\text{ m}}
$$

## 7. Tiling a floor

**Question:** A rectangular floor measures $4\text{ m}$ by $3.5\text{ m}$. Square tiles of side $0.5\text{ m}$ are used. How many tiles are needed?

**Method:** Area of floor $=4\times3.5=14\text{ m}^2$. Area of one tile $=0.5\times0.5=0.25\text{ m}^2$. Number of tiles $=14\div0.25=56$.

**Step 1 — Floor area.**

$$
4\times3.5=14\text{ m}^2
$$

**Step 2 — Tile area.**

$$
0.5\times0.5=0.25\text{ m}^2
$$

**Step 3 — Number of tiles.**

$$
14\div0.25=56
$$

$$
\boxed{56\text{ tiles}}
$$

\boxed{1.8\text{ m}^2}

## 10. Paint for a wall

**Question:** A rectangular wall is $6\text{ m}$ wide and $2.5\text{ m}$ high. One litre of paint covers $8\text{ m}^2$. How many litres are needed?

**Method:** Area of wall $=6\times2.5=15\text{ m}^2$. Litres needed $=15\div8=1.875$. Since paint is sold in whole litres, round up to 2 litres.

**Step 1 — Wall area.**

$$
6\times2.5=15\text{ m}^2
$$

**Step 2 — Litres.**

$$
15\div8=1.875
$$

**Step 3 — Round up.**

$$
\boxed{2\text{ litres}}
$$

# Geometry Basics: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Perimeter, Area and Heron's Formula**.

## Example 1: Perimeter of a rectangle

**Question:** Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Perimeter $=2(l+w)$.

**Step 1 — Add length and width.**

$$
12.5+8=20.5
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply by 2.**

$$
2\times20.5=41
$$

$$
\boxed{41\text{ cm}}
$$

## Example 2: Area of a rectangle

**Question:** Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Area $=l\times w$.

$$
12.5\times8=100
$$

$$
\boxed{100\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## Example 3: Area of a triangle

**Question:** Find the area of a triangle with base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Area $=\frac12\times b\times h$.

$$
\frac12\times10\times6=30
$$

$$
\boxed{30\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## Example 4: Heron's formula

**Question:** Find the area of a triangle with sides $5\text{ cm}, 6\text{ cm}, 7\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Heron's formula: $s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}$, Area $=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$.

**Step 1 — Semi-perimeter.**

$$
s=\frac{5+6+7}{2}=\frac{18}{2}=9
$$

**Step 2 — Area.**

$$
\text{Area}=\sqrt{9(9-5)(9-6)(9-7)}=\sqrt{9\times4\times3\times2}=\sqrt{216}=6\sqrt6
$$

$$
\boxed{6\sqrt6\text{ cm}^2\approx14.7\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## Example 5: Circumference of a circle

**Question:** Find the circumference of a circle of radius $7\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$).

**Method:** Circumference $=2\pi r$.

$$
2\times\frac{22}{7}\times7=44
$$

$$
\boxed{44\text{ cm}}
$$

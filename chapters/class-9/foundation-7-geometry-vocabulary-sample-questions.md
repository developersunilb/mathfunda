# Foundation 7: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Two angles on a straight line are $x$ and $125^\circ$. Find $x$.
2. Two angles of a triangle are $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. Find the third angle.
3. A triangle has side lengths 7 cm, 7 cm, 7 cm. Classify it.
4. A triangle has side lengths 6 cm, 6 cm, 10 cm. Classify it.
5. In which quadrant does $(-4,-5)$ lie?
6. In which quadrant does $(3,-2)$ lie?
7. Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(2,4)$ and $(8,10)$.
8. Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(-2,4)$ and $(4,-2)$.
9. A triangle has angles $35^\circ, 55^\circ$ and $90^\circ$. What kind of triangle is it by its angles?
10. Three angles of a quadrilateral are $85^\circ, 95^\circ$ and $110^\circ$. Find the fourth angle.
11. Two angles on a straight line are $70^\circ$ and $x$. Find $x$.
12. Two angles of a triangle are $50^\circ$ and $70^\circ$. Find the third angle.
13. A triangle has side lengths 5 cm, 12 cm, 13 cm. Classify it by its sides.
14. In which quadrant does $(-3,4)$ lie?
15. Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(0,0)$ and $(6,8)$.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

# Linear Equations: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Linear Equations in Two Variables**: tables of values, straight-line graphs, solving.

## Example 1: Table of values

**Question:** Complete a table of values for $y=2x+1$ at $x=0,1,2,3$.

**Method:** Substitute each $x$ into the equation.

| $x$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y=2x+1$ | $1$ | $3$ | $5$ | $7$ |

**Step 1 — Substitute.**

- $x=0:\ y=2(0)+1=1$
- $x=1:\ y=2(1)+1=3$
- $x=2:\ y=2(2)+1=5$
- $x=3:\ y=2(3)+1=7$

## Example 2: Straight-line graph

**Question:** Draw the graph of $y=2x+1$ and state its gradient and y-intercept.

**Method:** Plot the points $(0,1),(1,3),(2,5),(3,7)$ and join them with a straight line. The equation is in the form $y=mx+c$ where $m$ is the gradient and $c$ is the y-intercept.

- Gradient $m=2$
- y-intercept $c=1$ (the line crosses the y-axis at $(0,1)$)

$$
\boxed{\text{Gradient }2,\quad \text{y-intercept }1}
$$

## Example 3: Solve a linear equation in one variable

**Question:** Solve $3x+7=22$.

**Method:** Undo operations in reverse order.

**Step 1 — Subtract 7.**

$$
3x=15
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by 3.**

$$
x=5
$$

$$
\boxed{x=5}
$$

## Example 4: Find the equation from two points

**Question:** Find the equation of the line through $(1,3)$ and $(3,7)$.

**Method:** Gradient $m=\frac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}=\frac{7-3}{3-1}=\frac42=2$. Use $y=mx+c$ with point $(1,3)$: $3=2(1)+c\Rightarrow c=1$.

**Step 1 — Gradient.**

$$
m=\frac{7-3}{3-1}=2
$$

**Step 2 — y-intercept.**

$$
3=2(1)+c \Rightarrow c=1
$$

$$
\boxed{y=2x+1}
$$

## Example 5: Solution of a linear equation in two variables

**Question:** Is $(2,5)$ a solution of $y=2x+1$?

**Method:** Substitute $x=2$: $y=2(2)+1=5$, which matches the y-coordinate.

$$
\boxed{\text{Yes, }(2,5)\text{ is a solution}}
$$

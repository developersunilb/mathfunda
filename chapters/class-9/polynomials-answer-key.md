# Polynomials: Answer Key (Parent Only)

This page contains the answers and full solution methods for the **Practice Questions**. Keep it away from the student until they have attempted the questions.

> **How to use:** Ask your child to show their working for each question. Mark correct with a tick. For any wrong answer, go back to the first step where the method slipped and explain it using the method below — do not just give the final answer.

## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** $4x-9=0 \Rightarrow 4x=9 \Rightarrow x=\boxed{\frac94}$.

**Q2.** Remainder $=p(-1)=2(-1)^3+(-1)^2-3(-1)+5=-2+1+3+5=\boxed{7}$.

**Q3.** $p(1)=1-3+3-1=0$, so $\boxed{x-1\text{ is a factor}}$.

**Q4.** Highest exponent 3 $\rightarrow$ degree 3; three terms $\rightarrow$ $\boxed{\text{cubic trinomial}}$.

**Q5.** Product $ac=-210$, sum $11$: numbers $21,-10$. Split: $6x^2+21x-10x-35=3x(2x+7)-5(2x+7)=\boxed{(2x+7)(3x-5)}$.

**Q6.** $x^2-7x+12=(x-3)(x-4)$; set each factor to 0 $\Rightarrow \boxed{x=3\text{ or }x=4}$.

**Q7.** $(x+3)(x-2)=x^2-2x+3x-6=\boxed{x^2+x-6}$.

**Q8.** $9x^2-16=(3x)^2-4^2=\boxed{(3x-4)(3x+4)}$.

**Q9.** Remainder $=p(3)=3^3-4(3)^2+3+6=27-36+3+6=\boxed{0}$.

**Q10.** Product -12, sum -1: numbers $-4,+3$. Group: $x^2-4x+3x-12=x(x-4)+3(x-4)=\boxed{(x-4)(x+3)}$.

**Q11.** $(2x+5)^2=(2x)^2+2(2x)(5)+5^2=\boxed{4x^2+20x+25}$.

**Q12.** $p(2)=2^3-6(2)^2+11(2)-6=8-24+22-6=0$, so $\boxed{x=2\text{ is a zero}}$.

**Q13.** Product 6, sum 5: numbers $2,3$. $\boxed{(x+2)(x+3)}$.

**Q14.** $(3a-4)(3a+4)=(3a)^2-4^2=\boxed{9a^2-16}$.

**Q15.** $x-5=0\Rightarrow\boxed{x=5}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) using $p(1)$ instead of $p(-1)$ when dividing by $x+1$; (ii) choosing factors with the right product but wrong sum; (iii) forgetting to verify by expanding after factorising; (iv) confusing "zero of a polynomial" with the constant term. Revisit Polynomials if several of these appear.

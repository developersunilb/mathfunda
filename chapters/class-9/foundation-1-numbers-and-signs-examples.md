# Foundation 1: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Numbers and Signs**. Each example shows the full method step by step.

## Example 1: Compare two negative numbers

**Question:** Which is greater, $-7$ or $-3$?

**Step 1 — Locate both on the number line.** $-7$ is further left, and $-3$ is closer to zero.

**Step 2 — The number on the right is greater.**

$$
\boxed{-3 > -7}
$$

## Example 2: Add and subtract integers

**Question:** Simplify $-8 + 13 - 6$.

**Step 1 — Work from left to right.**

$$
-8 + 13 = 5
$$

**Step 2 — Subtract $6$.**

$$
5 - 6 = \boxed{-1}
$$

## Example 3: Subtract a negative

**Question:** Simplify $8 - (-3)$.

**Step 1 — Subtracting $-3$ is the same as adding $3$.**

$$
8 - (-3) = 8 + 3 = \boxed{11}
$$

## Example 4: Multiply and divide with signs

**Question:** Simplify $(-6)\times(+4)$ and $(-36)\div(-9)$.

**Step 1 — Different signs give a negative product.**

$$
(-6)\times(+4) = \boxed{-24}
$$

**Step 2 — Two negative signs give a positive quotient.**

$$
(-36)\div(-9) = \boxed{4}
$$

## Example 5: Use BODMAS

**Question:** Simplify $12 + 3\times(8-5)^2\div9$.

**Step 1 — Brackets.**

$$
12 + 3\times3^2\div9
$$

**Step 2 — Orders.**

$$
12 + 3\times9\div9
$$

**Step 3 — Multiplication and division from left to right.**

$$
12 + 27\div9 = 12 + 3
$$

**Step 4 — Addition.**

$$
\boxed{15}
$$

## Example 6: Absolute value

**Question:** Simplify $\lvert-9\rvert - \lvert4\rvert$.

**Step 1 — Evaluate each absolute value.**

$$
\lvert-9\rvert = 9,\qquad \lvert4\rvert = 4
$$

**Step 2 — Subtract.**

$$
9 - 4 = \boxed{5}
$$

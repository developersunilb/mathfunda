# Chapter 2: Introduction to Linear Polynomials

## 2.1 Linear polynomials

A linear polynomial has degree 1: $ax+b$, $a\ne0$.

## 2.2 Linear patterns

Patterns with constant difference are linear.

## 2.3 Input-output and graphs

Linear relationships graph as straight lines; slope is rate of change.

## Formula reference

| Form | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ax+b, a≠0 | Linear polynomial |

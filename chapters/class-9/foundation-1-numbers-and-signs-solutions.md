## 1. Temperature drop

**Question:** At 6 a.m. the temperature was $-4^\circ\text{C}$. By noon it had risen by $9^\circ\text{C}$, and by evening it fell by $7^\circ\text{C}$. What was the temperature in the evening?

**Method:** Start at $-4$, add $9$ (rise), then subtract $7$ (fall). Work left to right.

**Step 1 — Rise by $9$.**

$$
-4 + 9 = 5
$$

**Step 2 — Fall by $7$.**

$$
5 - 7 = -2
$$

$$
\boxed{-2^\circ\text{C}}
$$

The evening temperature was $2^\circ\text{C}$ below zero.

## 2. Bank account

**Question:** Meena's bank account shows a balance of $-\text{₹}500$ (overdrawn). She deposits $\text{₹}800$, then withdraws $\text{₹}300$. What is her final balance?

**Method:** A deposit adds to the balance; a withdrawal subtracts. Work left to right.

**Step 1 — Deposit $\text{₹}800$.**

$$
-500 + 800 = 300
$$

**Step 2 — Withdraw $\text{₹}300$.**

$$
300 - 300 = 0
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{₹}0}
$$

The account is exactly balanced — neither overdrawn nor in credit.

## 3. Buying and selling

**Question:** A shopkeeper buys an item for $\text{₹}120$ and sells it at a loss, so the selling price is $\text{₹}95$. Express the result as a signed change from the cost price, and find the loss.

**Method:** Signed change $=$ selling price $-$ cost price.

$$
95 - 120 = -25
$$

The signed change is $-\text{₹}25$ (a fall of $25$). The loss is $\boxed{\text{₹}25}$.

## 4. Hill and valley

**Question:** A point on a hill is $84\text{ m}$ above sea level, and a point in a valley is $37\text{ m}$ below sea level. Find the difference in height between the two points.

**Method:** Hill $=+84$, valley $=-37$. Difference $=$ higher $- $ lower.

$$
84 - (-37) = 84 + 37 = 121
$$

## 5. BODMAS in a receipt

**Question:** A receipt calculates $24\div3\times2+4$. A student worked it out as $24\div(3\times2+4) = 24\div10 = 2.4$. Is the student correct? If not, give the correct value using BODMAS.

**Method:** Division and multiplication have equal priority — work left to right before addition. The student wrongly forced the multiplication and addition inside the brackets first.

**Step 1 — Division first (it appears before multiplication).**

$$
24\div3 = 8
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply.**

$$
8\times2 = 16
$$

**Step 3 — Add.**

$$
16 + 4 = 20
$$

The student is **not correct**. The correct value is $\boxed{20}$.

## 6. Place value cheque

**Question:** A cheque is written for $5,06,742$. State the place value of the digit $6$ and the face value of the digit $5$.

**Method:** Place value $=$ digit $\times$ value of its place. Face value $=$ the digit itself.

- The $6$ is in the thousands place: $6\times1000 = \boxed{6000}$.
- The face value of $5$ is simply $\boxed{5}$.

## 7. Division algorithm

**Question:** A number leaves remainder $3$ when divided by $7$. Write it in the form $\text{dividend}=\text{divisor}\times\text{quotient}+\text{remainder}$, using quotient $5$.

**Method:** Substitute divisor $=7$, quotient $=5$, remainder $=3$.

$$
\text{dividend} = 7\times5 + 3 = 35 + 3 = 38
$$

$$
\boxed{38 = 7\times5 + 3}
$$

Check: $38\div7 = 5$ remainder $3$. ✓

## 8. Sign check

**Question:** Without calculating the full value, decide the sign of each result:
$(a)\ (-5)\times(-3)\times(-2)$
$(b)\ (-45)\div(+9)\times(-2)$

**Method:** Count the negative signs. An even number of negative signs gives a positive result; an odd number gives a negative result. Work left to right.

**(a)** $(-5)\times(-3)$: two negatives $\rightarrow$ positive $=15$. Then $15\times(-2)$: one negative $\rightarrow$ negative.

$$
\boxed{\text{(a) negative }(-30)}
$$

**(b)** $(-45)\div(+9)$: one negative $\rightarrow$ negative $=-5$. Then $(-5)\times(-2)$: two negatives $\rightarrow$ positive.

$$
\boxed{\text{(b) positive }(+10)}
$$

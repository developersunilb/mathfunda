## Questions

1. Convert $3.5\text{ km}$ to metres.
2. Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.
3. Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.
4. Find the area of a triangle with base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.
5. Convert $245\text{ cm}$ to metres.
6. Convert $5.2\text{ km}$ to metres.
7. A rectangular garden measures $15\text{ m}$ by $9\text{ m}$. Find the fencing needed (perimeter).
8. A rectangular floor measures $4\text{ m}$ by $3.5\text{ m}$. Square tiles of side $0.5\text{ m}$ — how many tiles?
9. A triangular banner has base $2.4\text{ m}$ and height $1.5\text{ m}$. Find its area.
10. Convert $85\text{ mm}$ to centimetres.
11. A rectangular wall is $6\text{ m}$ wide and $2.5\text{ m}$ high. One litre covers $8\text{ m}^2$. How many litres?
12. Find the perimeter of a rectangle $10\text{ cm}$ by $6\text{ cm}$.
13. Find the area of a rectangle $10\text{ cm}$ by $6\text{ cm}$.
14. Find the area of a triangle with base $8\text{ cm}$ and height $5\text{ cm}$.
15. Convert $7.3\text{ km}$ to metres.

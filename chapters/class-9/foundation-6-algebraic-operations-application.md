# Foundation 6: Application Questions

Application questions for **Algebraic Operations**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Area of a rectangle (algebraic)

A rectangle has length $(x+5)$ m and width $(x-2)$ m. Write an expression for its area and simplify.

## 2. Square garden

A square garden has side $(2x+3)$ m. Write an expression for its area and simplify.

## 3. Factorise a quadratic

Factorise $x^2+5x+6$.

## 4. Difference of squares

Factorise $16y^2-25$.

## 5. Simplify product

Simplify $(3a-4)(3a+4)$.

## 6. Expand and simplify

Expand and simplify $(x-7)(x+2)$.

## 7. Square of a binomial

Expand $(5t-3)^2$.

## 8. Factorise completely

Factorise $x^2-x-12$.

# Basic Equations: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Basic Equations**: keeping equations balanced and undoing operations in reverse order.

## Example 1: One-step equation

**Question:** Solve $3x+7=22$.

**Method:** Undo the operations in reverse order. Subtract 7, then divide by 3.

**Step 1 — Subtract 7 from both sides.**

$$
3x=22-7=15
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by 3.**

$$
x=\frac{15}{3}=5
$$

$$
\boxed{x=5}
$$

Check: $3(5)+7=15+7=22$. ✓

## Example 2: Equation with division

**Question:** Solve $\frac{x}{4}=9$.

**Method:** Undo the division by multiplying both sides by 4.

$$
x=9\times4=36
$$

$$
\boxed{x=36}
$$

Check: $\frac{36}{4}=9$. ✓

## Example 3: Two-step equation

**Question:** Solve $2x-5=11$.

**Method:** Undo subtraction first, then division.

**Step 1 — Add 5 to both sides.**

$$
2x=11+5=16
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by 2.**

$$
x=8
$$

$$
\boxed{x=8}
$$

Check: $2(8)-5=16-5=11$. ✓

## Example 4: Equation with brackets

**Question:** Solve $4(x-3)=20$.

**Method:** Divide by 4 first (undo the multiplication of the bracket), then add 3.

**Step 1 — Divide by 4.**

$$
x-3=5
$$

**Step 2 — Add 3.**

$$
x=8
$$

$$
\boxed{x=8}
$$

Check: $4(8-3)=4(5)=20$. ✓

## Example 5: Variable on both sides

**Question:** Solve $5x+3=3x+11$.

**Method:** Collect the $x$ terms on one side and constants on the other.

**Step 1 — Subtract $3x$ from both sides.**

$$
2x+3=11
$$

**Step 2 — Subtract 3.**

$$
2x=8
$$

**Step 3 — Divide by 2.**

$$
x=4
$$

$$
\boxed{x=4}
$$

Check: $5(4)+3=23$ and $3(4)+11=23$. ✓

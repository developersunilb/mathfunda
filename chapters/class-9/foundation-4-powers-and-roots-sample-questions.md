# Foundation 4: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Simplify $2^3\times2^2$.
2. Simplify $(3^2)^3$.
3. Find $\sqrt{144}$.
4. Simplify $\frac{5^7}{5^3}$.
5. Is $125$ a perfect cube? If so, find its cube root.
6. A square tile has side $12\text{ cm}$. Find its area using index notation.
7. A cube has edge $5\text{ cm}$. Find its volume using index notation.
8. A bacteria culture doubles every hour. Starting from 1 bacterium, how many after 8 hours? Write as a power of 2.
9. Simplify $\frac{2^6\times2^3}{2^4}$.
10. Find $\sqrt{225}$.
11. Find $\sqrt[3]{512}$.
12. Calculate $3^3+2^4-5^2$.
13. Simplify $4^3\times4^2$.
14. Simplify $\frac{6^5}{6^2}$.
15. Find $\sqrt[3]{1000}$.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

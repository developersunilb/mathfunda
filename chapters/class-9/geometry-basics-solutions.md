# Geometry Basics: Full Solutions

Full worked solutions for the **Application Questions**, including diagrams where helpful.

## 1. Perimeter of a rectangle

**Question:** Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Perimeter $=2(l+w)=2(12.5+8)$.

**Step 1 — Add.**

$$
12.5+8=20.5
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply by 2.**

$$
2\times20.5=41
$$

$$
\boxed{41\text{ cm}}
$$

## 2. Area of a rectangle

**Question:** Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Area $=l\times w=12.5\times8=100$.

$$
\boxed{100\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## 3. Area of a triangle

**Question:** Find the area of a triangle with base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** Area $=\frac12\times b\times h=\frac12\times10\times6=30$.

$$
\boxed{30\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## 4. Heron's formula

**Question:** Find the area of a triangle with sides $5\text{ cm}, 6\text{ cm}, 7\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** $s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}$, Area $=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$.

**Step 1 — Semi-perimeter.**

$$
s=\frac{5+6+7}{2}=9
$$

**Step 2 — Area.**

$$
\text{Area}=\sqrt{9(4)(3)(2)}=\sqrt{216}=6\sqrt6
$$

$$
\boxed{6\sqrt6\text{ cm}^2\approx14.7\text{ cm}^2}
$$

## 5. Circumference

**Question:** Find the circumference of a circle of radius $7\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$).

**Method:** $C=2\pi r=2\times\frac{22}{7}\times7=44$.

$$
\boxed{44\text{ cm}}
$$

## 6. Fencing a garden

**Question:** A rectangular garden measures $15\text{ m}$ by $9\text{ m}$. Find the fencing needed.

**Method:** Perimeter $=2(15+9)=2(24)=48$.

$$
\boxed{48\text{ m}}
$$

## 7. Tiling a floor

**Question:** A rectangular floor measures $4\text{ m}$ by $3.5\text{ m}$. Tiles of side $0.5\text{ m}$. How many tiles?

**Method:** Floor area $=4\times3.5=14\text{ m}^2$. Tile area $=0.5\times0.5=0.25\text{ m}^2$. Tiles $=14\div0.25=56$.

$$
\boxed{56\text{ tiles}}
$$

## 8. Triangle banner

**Question:** A triangular banner has base $2.4\text{ m}$ and height $1.5\text{ m}$. Find its area.

**Method:** Area $=\frac12\times2.4\times1.5=1.8$.

$$
\boxed{1.8\text{ m}^2}
$$

## 9. Heron's formula

**Question:** Find the area of a triangle with sides $6\text{ cm}, 8\text{ cm}, 10\text{ cm}$.

**Method:** $s=\frac{6+8+10}{2}=12$. Area $=\sqrt{12(12-6)(12-8)(12-10)}=\sqrt{12\times6\times4\times2}=\sqrt{576}=24$.

$$
\boxed{24\text{ cm}^2}
$$

Note: $6-8-10$ is a right triangle (doubled 3-4-5), so area also $=\frac12\times6\times8=24\text{ cm}^2$. ✓

## 10. Circle area

**Question:** Find the area of a circle of radius $14\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$).

**Method:** Area $=\pi r^2=\frac{22}{7}\times14^2=\frac{22}{7}\times196$.

**Step 1 — Square the radius.**

$$
14^2=196
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply.**

$$
\frac{22}{7}\times196=22\times28=616
$$

$$
\boxed{616\text{ cm}^2}
$$

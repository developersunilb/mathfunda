## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** $(5+2)+(3i-7i)=7-4i$.

**Q2.** $(6-1)+(-4i-2i)=5-6i$.

**Q3.** $(1+2i)(3+i)=3+i+6i+2i^2=3+7i-2=\boxed{1+7i}$.

**Q4.** $17\div4=4$ remainder 1 $\Rightarrow i^{17}=i^1=\boxed{i}$.

**Q5.** $22\div4=5$ remainder 2 $\Rightarrow i^{22}=i^2=\boxed{-1}$.

**Q6.** $45\times\frac{\pi}{180}=\boxed{\frac{\pi}{4}}$ radians.

**Q7.** $150\times\frac{\pi}{180}=\boxed{\frac{5\pi}{6}}$ radians.

**Q8.** $\frac{\pi}{6}\times\frac{180}{\pi}=\frac{180}{6}=\boxed{30^\circ}$.

**Q9.** $\frac{3\pi}{2}\times\frac{180}{\pi}=3\times90=\boxed{270^\circ}$.

**Q10.** $|3+4i|=\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{25}=\boxed{5}$.

**Q11.** Conjugate of $2-5i$ changes the sign of the imaginary part: $\boxed{2+5i}$.

**Q12.** $x^2=-4\Rightarrow x=\pm\sqrt{-4}=\pm\sqrt4\times i=\boxed{\pm2i}$.

**Q13.** $(3+4)+(2i-5i)=7-3i$.

**Q14.** $(2+3i)(1-4i)=2-8i+3i-12i^2=2-5i+12=\boxed{14-5i}$.

**Q15.** $23\div4=5$ remainder 3 $\Rightarrow i^{23}=i^3=\boxed{-i}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) forgetting $i^2=-1$ when multiplying complex numbers; (ii) dividing by negative when converting radians — actually multiply by $\frac{\pi}{180}$ for degrees→radians and $\frac{180}{\pi}$ for radians→degrees; (iii) wrong remainder for powers of $i$; (iv) writing $\sqrt{-4}=2$ instead of $2i$. Revisit Beyond Class 9 if several of these appear.

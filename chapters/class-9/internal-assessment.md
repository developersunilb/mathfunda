# Class 9 CBSE Mathematics — Internal Assessment (20 marks)

## Question Paper

### Periodic Test (10 marks)
Short quiz covering recent chapters.

### Notebook Assessment (5 marks)
Neat presentation, step-by-step working, corrections.

### Enrichment Activities (5 marks)
Algorithm tracing, vocabulary, formula recall, project work.

## Solutions and Marking Scheme

### Periodic Test (10)
Marked by teacher per school scheme.

### Notebook (5)
Rubric: neatness 2, working 2, corrections 1.

### Enrichment (5)
Rubric: participation 2, accuracy 2, presentation 1.

Total: 20.

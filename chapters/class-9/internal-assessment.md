# Class 9 CBSE Mathematics — Internal Assessment (20 marks)

## Structure
- Periodic Test (PT): 10 marks
- Notebook Assessment: 5 marks
- Enrichment Activities: 5 marks

## Periodic Test (10)
- Short quiz covering recent chapters (Part 2 priority first).
- Mix of objective and short-answer questions.

## Notebook Assessment (5)
- Neat presentation, step-by-step working, corrections.

## Enrichment (5)
- Algorithm tracing, vocabulary, formula recall, project work.

*Separate from 80-mark theory papers.*

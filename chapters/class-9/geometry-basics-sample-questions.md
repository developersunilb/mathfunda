# Geometry Basics: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.
2. Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.
3. Find the area of a triangle with base $10\text{ cm}$ and height $6\text{ cm}$.
4. Find the area of a triangle with sides $5\text{ cm}, 6\text{ cm}, 7\text{ cm}$ (Heron's formula).
5. Find the circumference of a circle of radius $7\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$).
6. A rectangular garden measures $15\text{ m}$ by $9\text{ m}$. Find the fencing needed.
7. A rectangular floor measures $4\text{ m}$ by $3.5\text{ m}$. Tiles of side $0.5\text{ m}$. How many tiles?
8. A triangular banner has base $2.4\text{ m}$ and height $1.5\text{ m}$. Find its area.
9. Find the area of a triangle with sides $6\text{ cm}, 8\text{ cm}, 10\text{ cm}$ (Heron's formula).
10. Find the area of a circle of radius $14\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$).
11. Find the perimeter of a rectangle $10\text{ cm}$ by $6\text{ cm}$.
12. Find the area of a rectangle $10\text{ cm}$ by $6\text{ cm}$.
13. Find the area of a triangle with base $8\text{ cm}$ and height $5\text{ cm}$.
14. Find the area of a triangle with sides $7\text{ cm}, 24\text{ cm}, 25\text{ cm}$ (Heron's formula).
15. Find the circumference of a circle of radius $14\text{ cm}$ (use $\pi=\frac{22}{7}$).

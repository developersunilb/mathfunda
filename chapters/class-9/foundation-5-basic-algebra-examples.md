# Foundation 5: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Basic Algebra**: like terms, expanding brackets, one-step equations.

## Example 1: Collect like terms

**Question:** Simplify $3x+5x-2x$.

**Method:** All terms contain $x$, so they are like terms. Combine the coefficients.

$$
(3+5-2)x=6x
$$

$$
\boxed{6x}
$$

## Example 2: Expand brackets

**Question:** Expand $4(2x-3)+5$.

**Method:** Distribute the 4 to both terms inside the bracket, then simplify.

**Step 1 — Distribute.**

$$
4\times2x-4\times3+5=8x-12+5
$$

**Step 2 — Combine like terms.**

$$
8x-7
$$

$$
\boxed{8x-7}
$$

## Example 3: Solve a one-step equation

**Question:** Solve $3x+7=22$.

**Method:** Undo the operations in reverse order. Subtract 7, then divide by 3.

**Step 1 — Subtract 7 from both sides.**

$$
3x=15
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by 3.**

$$
x=5
$$

$$
\boxed{x=5}
$$

## Example 4: Equation with division

**Question:** Solve $\frac{x}{4}=9$.

**Method:** Multiply both sides by 4.

$$
x=9\times4=36
$$

$$
\boxed{x=36}
$$

## Example 5: Expand and simplify two brackets

**Question:** Expand and simplify $2(x-5)+3(x+1)$.

**Method:** Distribute 2 and 3 separately, then collect like terms.

**Step 1 — Distribute.**

$$
2x-10+3x+3
$$

**Step 2 — Collect like terms.**

$$
(2x+3x)+(-10+3)=5x-7
$$

$$
\boxed{5x-7}
$$

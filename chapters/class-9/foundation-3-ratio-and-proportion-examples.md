# Foundation 3: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Ratio and Proportion**, including the unitary method.

## Example 1: Share in a ratio

**Question:** Divide £60 in the ratio 2:3.

**Method:** Total parts $=2+3=5$. One part $=60\div5=12$.

**Step 1 — One part.**

$$
60\div5=12
$$

**Step 2 — Each share.**

First: $2\times12=24$. Second: $3\times12=36$.

$$
\boxed{24\text{ and }36}
$$

Check: $24+36=60$. ✓

## Example 2: Unitary method

**Question:** 300 ml of red paint is mixed with blue paint in ratio 3:1. How much blue paint is needed?

**Method:** Red represents 3 parts $=300$ ml. One part $=300\div3=100$ ml. Blue is 1 part.

**Step 1 — One part.**

$$
300\div3=100\text{ ml}
$$

**Step 2 — Blue paint (1 part).**

$$
\boxed{100\text{ ml}}
$$

## Example 3: Simplify a ratio

**Question:** Simplify the ratio $18:24$.

**Method:** Divide both sides by their HCF. HCF of 18 and 24 is 6.

$$
18:24=(18\div6):(24\div6)=3:4
$$

$$
\boxed{3:4}
$$

## Example 4: Unitary cost

**Question:** 5 pens cost ₹75. Find the cost of 8 pens.

**Method:** Cost of 1 pen $=75\div5=15$. Cost of 8 pens $=15\times8=120$.

**Step 1 — Cost of 1 pen.**

$$
75\div5=15
$$

**Step 2 — Cost of 8 pens.**

$$
15\times8=120
$$

$$
\boxed{₹120}
$$

## Example 5: Direct proportion

**Question:** A car travels 120 km in 2 hours. How far in 7 hours at the same speed?

**Method:** Distance per hour $=120\div2=60$ km/h. In 7 hours: $60\times7=420$ km.

**Step 1 — Speed.**

$$
120\div2=60\text{ km/h}
$$

**Step 2 — Distance in 7 hours.**

$$
60\times7=420
$$

$$
\boxed{420\text{ km}}
$$

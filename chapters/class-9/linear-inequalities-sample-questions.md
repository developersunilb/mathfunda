# Linear Inequalities: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Solve $x+5>12$.
2. Solve $-2x<10$.
3. Solve $3x-4<8$.
4. Solve $5-2x\ge1$.
5. Show $x>3$ on a number line.
6. Show $x\le-2$ on a number line.
7. A student needs at least 40 marks to pass. She already has 28. How many more $m$ does she need?
8. A lift can carry at most 600 kg. Each box weighs 25 kg. Find the maximum number of boxes $b$.
9. Solve $4x+3>19$.
10. Solve $-3x\le9$.
11. Solve $x-7\ge2$.
12. Solve $-4x>12$.
13. Solve $2x+1<7$.
14. Solve $6-3x>0$.
15. A party needs at least 30 sandwiches. It already has 19. How many more $s$ does it need?

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

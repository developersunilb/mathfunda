## 1. Missing angle

**Question:** Two angles on a straight line are $x$ and $125^\circ$. Find $x$.

**Method:** Angles on a straight line add to $180^\circ$.

**Step 1 — Subtract from 180.**

$$
x=180-125=55
$$

$$
\boxed{x=55^\circ}
$$

## 2. Triangle third angle

**Question:** Two angles of a triangle are $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. Find the third angle.

**Method:** Angles in a triangle add to $180^\circ$.

**Step 1 — Subtract from 180.**

$$
180-48-67=65
$$

$$
\boxed{65^\circ}
$$

## 5. Quadrant

**Question:** In which quadrant does $(-4,-5)$ lie?

**Method:** $x<0$, $y<0$ $\rightarrow$ Quadrant III.

$$
\boxed{\text{Quadrant III}}
$$

## 7. Midpoint

**Question:** Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(2,4)$ and $(8,10)$.

**Method:** Average the coordinates.

**Step 1 — Average.**

$$
\left(\frac{2+8}{2},\frac{4+10}{2}\right)=\left(\frac{10}{2},\frac{14}{2}\right)=(5,7)
$$

$$
\boxed{(5,7)}
$$

## 8. Midpoint

**Question:** Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(-2,4)$ and $(4,-2)$.

**Method:** Average the coordinates.

**Step 1 — Average.**

$$
\left(\frac{-2+4}{2},\frac{4+(-2)}{2}\right)=\left(\frac{2}{2},\frac{2}{2}\right)=(1,1)
$$

$$
\boxed{(1,1)}
$$

## 9. Right angle check

**Method:** One angle is exactly $90^\circ$ $\rightarrow$ **right-angled** triangle.

$$
\boxed{\text{Right-angled triangle}}
$$

## 10. Angle sum of quadrilateral

**Question:** Three angles of a quadrilateral are $85^\circ, 95^\circ$ and $110^\circ$. Find the fourth angle.

**Method:** Angles in a quadrilateral add to $360^\circ$.

**Step 1 — Sum known angles.**

$$
85+95+110=290
$$

**Step 2 — Subtract from 360.**

$$
360-290=70
$$

$$
\boxed{70^\circ}
$$

# Number Systems: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Write $0.375$ as a fraction in simplest form.
2. Write $0.\overline{3}$ as a fraction.
3. Simplify $\frac{2^6\times2^3}{2^4}$.
4. Is $\sqrt{2}$ rational or irrational?
5. Find a rational number between $\frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{3}{4}$.
6. Write $0.6$ as a fraction in simplest form.
7. Write $0.\overline{7}$ as a fraction.
8. Simplify $5^2\times5^3$.
9. Simplify $(3^2)^3$.
10. Find a rational number between $\frac12$ and $\frac23$.
11. State whether $\frac78$ has a terminating or recurring decimal expansion.
12. Find an irrational number between 1 and 2.
13. Write $\frac38$ as a decimal.
14. Simplify $\frac{7^5}{7^2}$.
15. Find $\sqrt{144}$.
16. Between which two consecutive whole numbers does $\sqrt{10}$ lie?
17. State whether $0.101001000100001\ldots$ is rational or irrational.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

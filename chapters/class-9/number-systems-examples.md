# Number Systems: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Number Systems**: rational and irrational numbers, decimal expansions, laws of exponents.

## Example 1: Write as a fraction

**Question:** Write $0.375$ as a fraction in simplest form.

**Method:** Write over a power of 10, then cancel by the HCF.

**Step 1 — Write over $1000$.**

$$
0.375=\frac{375}{1000}
$$

**Step 2 — Cancel by HCF $125$.**

$$
\frac{375\div125}{1000\div125}=\frac{3}{8}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{3}{8}}
$$

## Example 2: Recurring decimal to fraction

**Question:** Write $0.\overline{3}$ as a fraction.

**Method:** Let $x=0.\overline{3}=0.333\ldots$. Multiply by 10 and subtract.

**Step 1 — Set up equations.**

$$
x=0.333\ldots,\qquad 10x=3.333\ldots
$$

**Step 2 — Subtract.**

$$
10x-x=3.333\ldots-0.333\ldots=3
$$

$$
9x=3 \Rightarrow x=\frac{1}{3}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{1}{3}}
$$

## Example 3: Laws of exponents

**Question:** Simplify $\frac{2^6\times2^3}{2^4}$.

**Method:** Numerator: add indices. Then divide: subtract indices.

**Step 1 — Numerator.**

$$
2^6\times2^3=2^{6+3}=2^9
$$

**Step 2 — Divide.**

$$
\frac{2^9}{2^4}=2^{9-4}=2^5=32
$$

$$
\boxed{32}
$$

## Example 4: Irrational number

**Question:** Is $\sqrt{2}$ rational or irrational?

**Method:** $\sqrt{2}$ cannot be written as a fraction $\frac{p}{q}$ with integers $p,q$; its decimal expansion is non-terminating and non-repeating ($1.41421356\ldots$).

$$
\boxed{\text{Irrational}}
$$

## Example 5: Insert a rational number between two rationals

**Question:** Find a rational number between $\frac{2}{3}$ and $\frac{3}{4}$.

**Method:** Take the average: $\frac12\left(\frac23+\frac34\right)$.

**Step 1 — Add.**

$$
\frac23+\frac34=\frac8{12}+\frac9{12}=\frac{17}{12}
$$

**Step 2 — Halve.**

$$
\frac12\times\frac{17}{12}=\frac{17}{24}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac{17}{24}}
$$

Check: $\frac23=\frac{16}{24}<\frac{17}{24}<\frac{18}{24}=\frac34$. ✓

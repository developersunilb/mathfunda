> **How to use:** Ask your child to show their working for each question. Mark correct with a tick. For any wrong answer, go back to the first step where the method slipped and explain it using the method below — do not just give the final answer.

## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** $1\text{ km}=1000\text{ m}$; $3.5\times1000=\boxed{3500\text{ m}}$.

**Q2.** Perimeter $=2(l+w)=2(12.5+8)=2(20.5)=\boxed{41\text{ cm}}$.

**Q3.** Area $=l\times w=12.5\times8=\boxed{100\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q4.** Area $=\frac12\times b\times h=\frac12\times10\times6=\boxed{30\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q5.** $100\text{ cm}=1\text{ m}$; $245\div100=\boxed{2.45\text{ m}}$.

**Q6.** $5.2\times1000=\boxed{5200\text{ m}}$.

**Q7.** Perimeter $=2(15+9)=2(24)=\boxed{48\text{ m}}$.

**Q8.** Floor area $=4\times3.5=14\text{ m}^2$; tile area $=0.5\times0.5=0.25\text{ m}^2$; tiles $=14\div0.25=\boxed{56}$.

**Q9.** Area $=\frac12\times2.4\times1.5=\boxed{1.8\text{ m}^2}$.

**Q10.** $10\text{ mm}=1\text{ cm}$; $85\div10=\boxed{8.5\text{ cm}}$.

**Q11.** Wall area $=6\times2.5=15\text{ m}^2$; litres $=15\div8=1.875$, round up $\rightarrow\boxed{2\text{ litres}}$.

**Q12.** Perimeter $=2(10+6)=2(16)=\boxed{32\text{ cm}}$.

**Q13.** Area $=10\times6=\boxed{60\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q14.** Area $=\frac12\times8\times5=\boxed{20\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q15.** $7.3\times1000=\boxed{7300\text{ m}}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) multiplying instead of dividing when converting cm→m or mm→cm; (ii) forgetting the factor of 2 in perimeter; (iii) forgetting $\frac12$ in triangle area; (iv) not rounding up when buying paint/tiles. Revisit Foundation 8 if several of these appear.

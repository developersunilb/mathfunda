# Polynomials: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Find the zero of $p(x)=4x-9$.
2. Find the remainder when $p(x)=2x^3+x^2-3x+5$ is divided by $x+1$.
3. Check whether $x-1$ is a factor of $p(x)=x^3-3x^2+3x-1$.
4. State the degree and type of $4x^3-2x+7$.
5. Factorise $6x^2+11x-35$.
6. Find the zeroes of $p(x)=x^2-7x+12$.
7. Expand $(x+3)(x-2)$.
8. Factorise $9x^2-16$.
9. Find the remainder when $p(x)=x^3-4x^2+x+6$ is divided by $x-3$.
10. Factorise $x^2-x-12$.
11. Expand $(2x+5)^2$.
12. Verify that $x=2$ is a zero of $p(x)=x^3-6x^2+11x-6$.
13. Factorise $x^2+5x+6$.
14. Simplify $(3a-4)(3a+4)$.
15. Find the zero of $p(x)=x-5$.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

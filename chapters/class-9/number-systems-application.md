# Number Systems: Application Questions

Application questions for **Number Systems**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Decimal to fraction

Write $0.6$ as a fraction in simplest form.

## 2. Recurring decimal

Write $0.\overline{7}$ as a fraction.

## 3. Laws of exponents

Simplify $5^2\times5^3$.

## 4. Laws of exponents

Simplify $(3^2)^3$.

## 5. Rational between two rationals

Find a rational number between $\frac{1}{2}$ and $\frac{2}{3}$.

## 6. Terminating or recurring

Without dividing, state whether $\frac{7}{8}$ has a terminating or recurring decimal expansion.

## 7. Irrational between two rationals

Find an irrational number between $1$ and $2$.

## 8. Decimal expansion

Write $\frac{3}{8}$ as a decimal.

## 9. Simplify indices

Simplify $\frac{7^5}{7^2}$.

## 10. Square root of a perfect square

Find $\sqrt{144}$.

## 11. Estimate an irrational

$\sqrt{10}$ lies between which two consecutive whole numbers?

## 12. Rational or irrational

State whether $0.101001000100001\ldots$ is rational or irrational, and why.

# Foundation 3: Application Questions

Application questions for **Ratio and Proportion**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Mixing paint

Pink paint is made by mixing red and white in the ratio 2:5. How much red and white paint are needed to make 350 ml of pink paint?

## 2. Sharing money

₹900 is shared between A, B and C in the ratio 2:3:4. How much does each person receive?

## 3. Recipe for cookies

A cookie recipe uses flour and butter in the ratio 4:1. If 600 g of flour is used, how much butter is needed?

## 4. Map scale

A map scale is 1:50,000. Two towns are 6 cm apart on the map. What is the actual distance in kilometres?

## 5. Unitary cost

8 notebooks cost ₹240. Find the cost of 15 notebooks.

## 6. Speed and time

A cyclist covers 36 km in 3 hours. How long to cover 60 km at the same speed?

## 7. Ratio of ages

The ratio of ages of a father and son is 7:3. If the son is 15 years old, how old is the father?

## 8. Comparing ratios

A class has 18 boys and 12 girls. Write the ratio of boys to girls in simplest form.

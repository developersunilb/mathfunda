# Chapter 6: Measuring Space — Perimeter and Area

## 6.1 Perimeter

Perimeter is the distance around a shape.

## 6.2 Area

Area is the space enclosed by a shape.

## 6.3 Rectangle, parallelogram, triangle

- Rectangle: $A = l \times b$.
- Parallelogram: $A = \text{base} \times \text{height}$.
- Triangle: $A = \frac12 \times \text{base} \times \text{height}$.

## 6.4 Heron's formula

For sides $a,b,c$ with $s=\frac{a+b+c}{2}$: $A=\sqrt{s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)}$.

## 6.5 Circle area and sector

$A = \pi r^2$; sector area $= \frac{\theta}{360}\pi r^2$.

## Formula reference

| Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|
| A rectangle = l×b | Area |
| A triangle = 1/2 base×height | Area |
| Heron: √[s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)] | Area |

# Basic Equations: Full Solutions

Full worked solutions for the **Application Questions**, including diagrams where helpful.

## 1. Number puzzle

**Question:** A number is doubled and 7 is added, giving 25. Find the number.

**Method:** Let the number be $x$. Equation $2x+7=25$. Undo: subtract 7, then divide by 2.

**Step 1 — Subtract 7.**

$$
2x=25-7=18
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by 2.**

$$
x=9
$$

$$
\boxed{9}
$$

Check: double 9 is 18, add 7 gives 25. ✓

## 2. Buying pens

**Question:** Pens cost ₹$p$ each. Buying 6 pens costs ₹72. Find the cost of one pen.

**Method:** Equation $6p=72$. Divide by 6.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
6p=72
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by 6.**

$$
p=12
$$

$$
\boxed{₹12\text{ per pen}}
$$

## 3. Ages

**Question:** Sarah is $x$ years old. Her brother is 3 years older. Their combined age is 27. Find Sarah's age.

**Method:** Brother's age $=x+3$. Combined age $=x+(x+3)=27$.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
x+x+3=27
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
2x+3=27
$$

$$
2x=24
$$

$$
x=12
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{Sarah is 12 years old}}
$$

Check: Sarah 12, brother 15, total 27. ✓

## 4. Garden fence

**Question:** A rectangular garden has width $w$ m and length $w+3$ m. The perimeter is 30 m. Find the width.

**Method:** Perimeter $=2(w+w+3)=30$.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
2(w+w+3)=30
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
4w+6=30
$$

$$
4w=24
$$

$$
w=6
$$

$$
\boxed{w=6\text{ m}}
$$

Check: width 6 m, length 9 m, perimeter $2(6+9)=30$ m. ✓

## 5. Triangle angles

**Question:** Two angles of a triangle are $x$ and $x+20$, and the third is $90^\circ$. Find $x$.

**Method:** Angles in a triangle add to $180^\circ$: $x+(x+20)+90=180$.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
x+x+20+90=180
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
2x+110=180
$$

$$
2x=70
$$

$$
x=35
$$

$$
\boxed{x=35^\circ}
$$

Check: angles $35^\circ,55^\circ,90^\circ$; sum $=180^\circ$. ✓

## 7. Sweets shared

**Question:** A box of sweets is shared equally among 5 children, and each gets 8 sweets. How many sweets were in the box?

**Method:** Let the total be $s$. Equation $s\div5=8$, i.e. $\frac{s}{5}=8$. Multiply by 5.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
\frac{s}{5}=8
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply by 5.**

$$
s=40
$$

$$
\boxed{40\text{ sweets}}
$$

## 8. Triangle perimeter

**Question:** A triangle has sides $x$, $x+3$ and $x+5$ cm. The perimeter is 36 cm. Find $x$.

**Method:** Perimeter $=x+(x+3)+(x+5)=36$.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
x+x+3+x+5=36
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
3x+8=36
$$

$$
3x=28
$$

$$
x=\frac{28}{3}=9\frac13
$$

$$
\boxed{x=9\frac13\text{ cm}}
$$

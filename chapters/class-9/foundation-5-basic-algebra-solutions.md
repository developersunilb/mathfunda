## 1. Perimeter expression

**Question:** A rectangle has length $(x+4)$ cm and width $x$ cm. Write an expression for its perimeter and simplify.

**Method:** Perimeter $=2(\text{length}+\text{width})$.

**Step 1 — Add length and width.**

$$
(x+4)+x=2x+4
$$

**Step 2 — Multiply by 2.**

$$
2(2x+4)=4x+8
$$

$$
\boxed{4x+8\text{ cm}}
$$

## 2. Total cost

**Question:** A notebook costs ₹$x$ and a pen costs ₹5 less than a notebook. Write an expression for the cost of 3 notebooks and 2 pens, and simplify.

**Method:** Pen cost $=x-5$. Total $=3x+2(x-5)$.

**Step 1 — Write expression.**

$$
3x+2(x-5)
$$

**Step 2 — Expand and simplify.**

$$
3x+2x-10=5x-10
$$

$$
\boxed{5x-10\text{ rupees}}
$$

## 3. Number puzzle

**Question:** A number is doubled and 7 is added, giving 25. Find the number.

**Method:** Let the number be $x$. Equation: $2x+7=25$. Undo: subtract 7, then divide by 2.

**Step 1 — Subtract 7.**

$$
2x=25-7=18
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by 2.**

$$
x=9
$$

$$
\boxed{9}
$$

Check: double 9 is 18, add 7 gives 25. ✓

## 4. Ages

**Question:** Sarah is $x$ years old. Her brother is 3 years older. Their combined age is 27. Write an equation and find Sarah's age.

**Method:** Brother's age $=x+3$. Combined: $x+(x+3)=27$.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
x+x+3=27
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
2x+3=27
$$

$$
2x=24
$$

$$
x=12
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{Sarah is 12 years old}}
$$

Check: Sarah 12, brother 15, total 27. ✓

## 5. Garden fence

**Question:** A rectangular garden has width $w$ metres and length 3 metres more than the width. The perimeter is 30 m. Write an equation and find the width.

**Method:** Length $=w+3$. Perimeter $=2(w+(w+3))=30$.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
2(w+w+3)=30
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
2(2w+3)=30
$$

$$
4w+6=30
$$

$$
4w=24
$$

$$
w=6
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{Width }=6\text{ m}}
$$

Check: length $9$ m, perimeter $2(6+9)=30$ m. ✓

## 6. Buying pens

**Question:** Pens cost ₹$p$ each. Buying 6 pens costs ₹72. Write an equation and find the cost of one pen.

**Method:** Equation $6p=72$. Divide by 6.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
6p=72
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by 6.**

$$
p=12
$$

$$
\boxed{₹12\text{ per pen}}
$$

## 7. Triangle angles

**Question:** Two angles of a triangle are $x$ and $x+20$, and the third is $90$. Write an equation for the angle sum and find $x$.

**Method:** Angles in a triangle add to $180$: $x+(x+20)+90=180$.

**Step 1 — Write equation.**

$$
x+x+20+90=180
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
2x+110=180
$$

$$
2x=70
$$

$$
x=35
$$

$$
\boxed{x=35^\circ}
$$

Check: angles $35^\circ,55^\circ,90^\circ$; sum $=180^\circ$. ✓

## 8. Simplify expression

**Question:** Simplify $5(2a-3)-2(a+4)$.

**Method:** Distribute 5 and $-2$, then collect like terms.

**Step 1 — Distribute.**

$$
10a-15-2a-8
$$

**Step 2 — Collect like terms.**

$$
(10a-2a)+(-15-8)=8a-23
$$

$$
\boxed{8a-23}
$$

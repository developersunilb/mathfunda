# Chapter 8: Predicting What Comes Next — Exploring Sequences and Progressions

## 8.1 Sequences

A sequence is an ordered list of numbers.

## 8.2 Arithmetic progression

AP: each term differs by common difference $d$. $n$-th term: $a_n = a + (n-1)d$. Sum: $S_n = \frac n2(2a + (n-1)d)$.

## 8.3 Geometric progression

GP: each term multiplied by common ratio $r$. $n$-th term: $a_n = a r^{n-1}$.

## 8.4 Sum of first n natural numbers

$1+2+\dots+n = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$.

## Formula reference

| Formula | Meaning |
|---|---|
| a_n = a+(n-1)d | AP nth term |
| S_n = n/2(2a+(n-1)d) | AP sum |
| 1+...+n = n(n+1)/2 | Sum naturals |

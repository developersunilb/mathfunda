# Polynomials: Application Questions

Application questions for **Polynomials**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Zero of a polynomial

Find the zero of $p(x)=4x-9$.

## 2. Remainder theorem

Find the remainder when $p(x)=2x^3+x^2-3x+5$ is divided by $x+1$.

## 3. Factor theorem

Check whether $x-1$ is a factor of $p(x)=x^3-3x^2+3x-1$.

## 4. Degree and type

State the degree and type of $4x^3-2x+7$.

## 5. Factorise

Factorise $6x^2+11x-35$.

## 6. Zero of a quadratic

Find the zeroes of $p(x)=x^2-7x+12$.

## 7. Identity — expand

Expand $(x+3)(x-2)$.

## 8. Identity — difference of squares

Factorise $9x^2-16$.

## 9. Remainder theorem

Find the remainder when $p(x)=x^3-4x^2+x+6$ is divided by $x-3$.

## 10. Factorise completely

Factorise $x^2-x-12$.

## 11. Algebraic identity

Expand $(2x+5)^2$.

## 12. Verify zero

Verify that $x=2$ is a zero of $p(x)=x^3-6x^2+11x-6$.

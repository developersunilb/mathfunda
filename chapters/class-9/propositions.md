# Chapter 9: Propositions and their Converses

## 9.1 Proposition

A proposition is a statement that is either true or false.

## 9.2 Converse

The converse of "If P then Q" is "If Q then P". A true proposition may have a false converse.

## 9.3 Implication and counterexample

An implication is proven by logic; disproven by a single counterexample.

## 9.4 Proof versus disproof

Proof requires general reasoning; disproof requires one concrete counterexample.

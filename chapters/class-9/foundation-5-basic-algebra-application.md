# Foundation 5: Application Questions

Application questions for **Basic Algebra**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Perimeter expression

A rectangle has length $(x+4)$ cm and width $x$ cm. Write an expression for its perimeter and simplify.

## 2. Total cost

A notebook costs ₹$x$ and a pen costs ₹5 less than a notebook. Write an expression for the cost of 3 notebooks and 2 pens, and simplify.

## 3. Number puzzle

A number is doubled and 7 is added, giving 25. Find the number.

## 4. Ages

Sarah is $x$ years old. Her brother is 3 years older. Their combined age is 27. Write an equation and find Sarah's age.

## 5. Garden fence

A rectangular garden has width $w$ metres and length 3 metres more than the width. The perimeter is 30 m. Write an equation and find the width.

## 6. Buying pens

Pens cost ₹$p$ each. Buying 6 pens costs ₹72. Write an equation and find the cost of one pen.

## 7. Triangle angles

Two angles of a triangle are $x$ and $x+20$, and the third is $90$. Write an equation for the angle sum and find $x$.

## 8. Simplify expression

Simplify $5(2a-3)-2(a+4)$.

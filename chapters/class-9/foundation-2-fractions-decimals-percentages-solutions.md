## 1. Recipe scaling

**Question:** A recipe for 6 people needs $\frac{3}{4}$ kg of flour. How much flour is needed for 8 people?

**Method:** Find flour per person (unitary method), then multiply by 8.

**Step 1 — Flour for 1 person.**

$$
\frac{3}{4}\div6=\frac{3}{4}\times\frac{1}{6}=\frac{3}{24}=\frac{1}{8}\text{ kg}
$$

**Step 2 — Flour for 8 people.**

$$
\frac{1}{8}\times8=1\text{ kg}
$$

$$
\boxed{1\text{ kg}}
$$

## 2. Discount

**Question:** A jacket costs $\text{₹}800$ and is sold at $25\%$ off. How much is taken off, and what is the sale price?

**Method:** Discount amount $=25\%\times800$. Sale price $=$ original $-$ discount.

**Step 1 — Discount.**

$$
25\%=\frac14,\qquad \frac14\times800=\text{₹}200
$$

**Step 2 — Sale price.**

$$
800-200=\text{₹}600
$$

$$
\boxed{\text{Discount }\text{₹}200;\quad \text{sale price }\text{₹}600}
$$

## 3. Sharing a sum

**Question:** $\text{₹}720$ is divided between two charities in the ratio $3:5$. How much does each charity receive?

**Method:** Total parts $=3+5=8$. One part $=720\div8=90$.

**Step 1 — One part.**

$$
720\div8=90
$$

**Step 2 — Each share.**

First charity: $3\times90=\text{₹}270$. Second charity: $5\times90=\text{₹}450$.

## 4. Fuel consumption

**Question:** A car uses $5.6$ litres to travel $40\text{ km}$. How many litres for $100\text{ km}$ at the same rate?

**Method:** Litres per km (unitary), then multiply by 100.

**Step 1 — Litres per km.**

$$
5.6\div40=0.14\text{ litre/km}
$$

**Step 2 — For 100 km.**

$$
0.14\times100=14\text{ litres}
$$

$$
\boxed{14\text{ litres}}
$$

## 5. Test score

**Question:** Riya scored $42$ out of $50$. Express her score as a percentage.

**Method:** Percentage $=\frac{42}{50}\times100\%$.

**Step 1 — Convert denominator to $100$.**

$$
\frac{42}{50}=\frac{42\times2}{50\times2}=\frac{84}{100}=84\%
$$

$$
\boxed{84\%}
$$

## 6. Fraction of an hour

**Question:** Express $45$ minutes as a fraction of an hour, in simplest form.

**Method:** $1\text{ hour}=60\text{ minutes}$. Fraction $=\frac{45}{60}$.

**Step 1 — Simplify by HCF $15$.**

$$
\frac{45}{60}=\frac{45\div15}{60\div15}=\frac{3}{4}
$$

$$
\boxed{\frac34\text{ of an hour}}
$$

## 7. Decimal division

**Question:** A $4.8\text{ m}$ ribbon is cut into pieces of $0.6\text{ m}$ each. How many pieces?

**Method:** Number of pieces $=4.8\div0.6$. Convert to whole-number division by multiplying both by 10.

**Step 1 — Make the divisor a whole number.**

$$
4.8\div0.6=48\div6=8
$$

$$
\boxed{8\text{ pieces}}
$$

## 8. Mixed operation

**Question:** Calculate $0.375\times\frac{4}{3}+25\%\text{ of }80$.

**Method:** Multiplication and percentage before addition (BODMAS).

**Step 1 — $0.375\times\frac{4}{3}$.**

$$
0.375=\frac{3}{8},\qquad \frac{3}{8}\times\frac{4}{3}=\frac{12}{24}=\frac12=0.5
$$

**Step 2 — $25\%$ of $80$.**

$$
\frac14\times80=20
$$

**Step 3 — Add.**

$$
0.5+20=20.5
$$

$$
\boxed{20.5}
$$

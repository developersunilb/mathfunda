## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** Perimeter $=2(l+w)=2(12.5+8)=2(20.5)=\boxed{41\text{ cm}}$.

**Q2.** Area $=l\times w=12.5\times8=\boxed{100\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q3.** Area $=\frac12\times b\times h=\frac12\times10\times6=\boxed{30\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q4.** $s=\frac{5+6+7}{2}=9$; Area $=\sqrt{9(4)(3)(2)}=\sqrt{216}=6\sqrt6\approx\boxed{14.7\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q5.** Circumference $=2\pi r=2\times\frac{22}{7}\times7=\boxed{44\text{ cm}}$.

**Q6.** Perimeter $=2(15+9)=2(24)=\boxed{48\text{ m}}$.

**Q7.** Floor area $=4\times3.5=14\text{ m}^2$; tile area $=0.5\times0.5=0.25\text{ m}^2$; tiles $=14\div0.25=\boxed{56}$.

**Q8.** Area $=\frac12\times2.4\times1.5=\boxed{1.8\text{ m}^2}$.

**Q9.** $s=\frac{6+8+10}{2}=12$; Area $=\sqrt{12(6)(4)(2)}=\sqrt{576}=\boxed{24\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q10.** Area $=\pi r^2=\frac{22}{7}\times14^2=\frac{22}{7}\times196=22\times28=\boxed{616\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q11.** Perimeter $=2(10+6)=2(16)=\boxed{32\text{ cm}}$.

**Q12.** Area $=10\times6=\boxed{60\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q13.** Area $=\frac12\times8\times5=\boxed{20\text{ cm}^2}$.

**Q14.** $s=\frac{7+24+25}{2}=28$; Area $=\sqrt{28(28-7)(28-24)(28-25)}=\sqrt{28\times21\times4\times3}=\sqrt{7056}=\boxed{84\text{ cm}^2}$. (7-24-25 is a right triangle: $\frac12\times7\times24=84$. ✓)

**Q15.** Circumference $=2\times\frac{22}{7}\times14=2\times22\times2=\boxed{88\text{ cm}}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) using diameter instead of radius in $2\pi r$ or $\pi r^2$; (ii) forgetting $\frac12$ in triangle area; (iii) arithmetic errors in Heron's formula (especially $s-a$, $s-b$, $s-c$); (iv) confusing perimeter with area units. Revisit Geometry Basics if several of these appear.

## Worked Reasoning for All Questions

**Q1.** Calculate $348\div12$.

**Answer:** $29$

**Reasoning:** $12\times20=240$, $348-240=108$, $12\times9=108$, so $20+9=29$. Or long division: $34\div12=2$ remainder $10$, bring down $8$ to make $108$, $108\div12=9$.

---

**Q2.** Simplify $-8+13-6$.

**Answer:** $-1$

**Reasoning:** Start at $-8$, move $13$ steps right to $5$, then $6$ steps left to $-1$. So $-8+13-6=5-6=-1$.

---

**Q3.** Find $\frac{2}{3}+\frac{3}{4}$.

**Answer:** $\frac{17}{12}=1\frac{5}{12}$

**Reasoning:** LCM of $3$ and $4$ is $12$. $\frac{2}{3}=\frac{8}{12}$ and $\frac{3}{4}=\frac{9}{12}$. So $\frac{8}{12}+\frac{9}{12}=\frac{17}{12}=1\frac{5}{12}$.

---

**Q4.** Calculate $3.75\times0.4$.

**Answer:** $1.5$

**Reasoning:** Multiply as whole numbers: $375\times4=1500$. Count decimal places: $2$ in $3.75$ and $1$ in $0.4$, so $3$ places total. $1500$ becomes $1.500=1.5$.

---

**Q5.** Find $15\%$ of $240$.

**Answer:** $36$

**Reasoning:** $15\%=\frac{15}{100}=0.15$. So $0.15\times240=36$.

---

**Q6.** Divide $360$ in the ratio $2:3$.

**Answer:** $144$ and $216$

**Reasoning:** The ratio has $2+3=5$ equal parts. One part is $360\div5=72$. So the shares are $2\times72=144$ and $3\times72=216$.

---

**Q7.** Find the HCF and the LCM of $18$ and $24$.

**Answer:** HCF $=6$, LCM $=72$

**Reasoning:** $18=2\times3^2$ and $24=2^3\times3$. The HCF uses the lowest powers: $2\times3=6$. The LCM uses the highest powers: $2^3\times3^2=8\times9=72$.

---

**Q8.** Simplify $2^3\times2^2$.

**Answer:** $32$

**Reasoning:** Same base, add indices: $2^3\times2^2=2^{3+2}=2^5=32$.

---

**Q9.** Simplify $3x+5x-2x$.

**Answer:** $6x$

**Reasoning:** All terms are like terms (same variable $x$). $(3+5-2)x=6x$.

---

**Q10.** Solve $3x+7=22$.

**Answer:** $x=5$

**Reasoning:** Subtract $7$ from both sides: $3x=15$. Divide by $3$: $x=5$.

---

**Q11.** Two angles of a triangle are $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. Find the third angle.

**Answer:** $65^\circ$

**Reasoning:** Angles in a triangle add to $180^\circ$. So $180^\circ-48^\circ-67^\circ=65^\circ$.

---

**Q12.** Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Answer:** $100\text{ cm}^2$

**Reasoning:** Area $=\text{length}\times\text{width}=12.5\times8=100\text{ cm}^2$.

---

**Q13.** Simplify $(-3)\times(-4)+(-2)$.

**Answer:** $10$

**Reasoning:** Multiplication first: $(-3)\times(-4)=12$ (negative times negative is positive). Then add: $12+(-2)=12-2=10$.

---

**Q14.** Find $\frac{5}{6}\div\frac{2}{3}$.

**Answer:** $\frac{5}{4}=1\frac{1}{4}$

**Reasoning:** Dividing by $\frac{2}{3}$ is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal $\frac{3}{2}$. So $\frac{5}{6}\times\frac{3}{2}=\frac{15}{12}=\frac{5}{4}=1\frac{1}{4}$.

---

**Q15.** Write $0.375$ as a fraction in its simplest form.

**Answer:** $\frac{3}{8}$

**Reasoning:** $0.375=\frac{375}{1000}$. Divide numerator and denominator by HCF $125$: $\frac{375\div125}{1000\div125}=\frac{3}{8}$.

---

**Q16.** Expand and simplify $4(2x-3)+5$.

**Answer:** $8x-7$

**Reasoning:** Multiply each term inside the bracket by $4$: $8x-12$. Then add $5$: $8x-12+5=8x-7$.

---

**Q17.** Solve $\frac{x}{4}=9$.

**Answer:** $x=36$

**Reasoning:** Multiply both sides by $4$: $x=9\times4=36$.

---

**Q18.** Round $4.678$ to two decimal places, and state the place value of the digit $7$.

**Answer:** $4.68$; hundredths

**Reasoning:** The third decimal digit is $8$, so round the hundredths digit ($7$) up to $8$: $4.68$. In $4.678$ the $7$ is in the hundredths place, so its place value is $\frac{7}{100}=0.07$.

---

**Q19.** Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Answer:** $41\text{ cm}$

**Reasoning:** Perimeter $=2(l+w)=2(12.5+8)=2(20.5)=41\text{ cm}$.

---

**Q20.** In which quadrant does $(-3,4)$ lie? Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(2,4)$ and $(8,10)$.

**Answer:** Quadrant II; $(5,7)$

**Reasoning:** In $(-3,4)$ the $x$-coordinate is negative and the $y$-coordinate is positive, so the point lies in Quadrant II. The midpoint is $\left(\frac{2+8}{2},\frac{4+10}{2}\right)=(5,7)$.

## 3. Marking and Diagnosis

Give **1 mark per question**. Do not deduct marks for a correct method with a small arithmetic slip; instead, mark the method separately as a note.

| Score out of 20 | What it suggests | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 16–20 | Foundations are largely secure. | Quick revision, then begin Chapter 1 of the study plan. |
| 11–15 | Some gaps are blocking new work. | Repair the weak rows in the table below, then continue. |
| 6–10 | Several foundation skills need rebuilding. | Spend Days 2–5 on the foundation lessons before Chapter 1. |
| 0–5 | Start gently and rebuild confidence. | Work through the foundation lessons slowly with a parent, one small idea at a time. |

> **Important:** Do not judge by the total alone. A student can score 16 and still have a serious fraction gap that will affect mensuration and algebra. Use the skill table.

## 5. After the Diagnostic

1. Write the numbers of the Red questions at the top of the mistakes notebook.
2. Work through the matching foundation lesson.
3. Retry those exact questions after two days, without looking at the key.
4. Then continue with the 45-day plan from Day 2.

Speed is not the goal yet. Accuracy and understanding come first; speed follows from repeated, correct practice.

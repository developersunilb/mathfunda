## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** $x+5>12 \Rightarrow x>12-5 \Rightarrow \boxed{x>7}$.

**Q2.** $-2x<10 \Rightarrow$ divide by $-2$, flip sign: $x>\boxed{-5}$.

**Q3.** $3x-4<8 \Rightarrow 3x<12 \Rightarrow \boxed{x<4}$.

**Q4.** $5-2x\ge1 \Rightarrow -2x\ge-4 \Rightarrow$ divide by $-2$, flip: $\boxed{x\le2}$.

**Q5.** Open circle at 3 (not included), shade to the right.

**Q6.** Closed circle at -2 (included), shade to the left.

**Q7.** $28+m\ge40 \Rightarrow m\ge40-28 \Rightarrow \boxed{m\ge12}$.

**Q8.** $25b\le600 \Rightarrow b\le600\div25=24$. $\boxed{b\le24;\text{ max }24\text{ boxes}}$.

**Q9.** $4x+3>19 \Rightarrow 4x>16 \Rightarrow \boxed{x>4}$.

**Q10.** $-3x\le9 \Rightarrow$ divide by $-3$, flip: $\boxed{x\ge-3}$.

**Q11.** $x-7\ge2 \Rightarrow \boxed{x\ge9}$.

**Q12.** $-4x>12 \Rightarrow$ divide by $-4$, flip: $\boxed{x<-3}$.

**Q13.** $2x+1<7 \Rightarrow 2x<6 \Rightarrow \boxed{x<3}$.

**Q14.** $6-3x>0 \Rightarrow -3x>-6 \Rightarrow$ divide by $-3$, flip: $\boxed{x<2}$.

**Q15.** $19+s\ge30 \Rightarrow s\ge30-19 \Rightarrow \boxed{s\ge11}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) forgetting to flip the sign when multiplying/dividing by a negative; (ii) using a closed circle for $>$ or $<$ (should be open) and vice versa; (iii) shading the wrong direction on the number line; (iv) confusing "at least" ($\ge$) with "at most" ($\le$). Revisit Linear Inequalities if several of these appear.

> **How to use:** Ask your child to show their working for each question. Mark correct with a tick. For any wrong answer, go back to the first step where the method slipped and explain it using the method below — do not just give the final answer.

## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** Total parts $2+3=5$. One part $60\div5=12$. Shares $2\times12=\boxed{£24}$, $3\times12=\boxed{£36}$.

**Q2.** Red is 3 parts $=300$ ml. One part $=300\div3=100$ ml. Blue is 1 part $=\boxed{100\text{ ml}}$.

**Q3.** HCF of 18 and 24 is 6. $18:24=(18\div6):(24\div6)=\boxed{3:4}$.

**Q4.** Cost of 1 pen $=75\div5=15$. Cost of 8 pens $=15\times8=\boxed{₹120}$.

**Q5.** Distance per hour $=120\div2=60$ km/h. In 7 hours: $60\times7=\boxed{420\text{ km}}$.

**Q6.** Total parts $2+5=7$. One part $=350\div7=50$ ml. Red $2\times50=\boxed{100\text{ ml}}$, white $5\times50=\boxed{250\text{ ml}}$.

**Q7.** Total parts $2+3+4=9$. One part $=900\div9=100$. A $2\times100=\boxed{₹200}$, B $3\times100=\boxed{₹300}$, C $4\times100=\boxed{₹400}$.

**Q8.** Flour 4 parts $=600$ g. One part $=600\div4=150$ g. Butter 1 part $=\boxed{150\text{ g}}$.

**Q9.** 1 cm $=50{,}000$ cm. $6\times50{,}000=300{,}000$ cm $=300{,}000\div100{,}000=\boxed{3\text{ km}}$.

**Q10.** Cost of 1 notebook $=240\div8=30$. Cost of 15 $=30\times15=\boxed{₹450}$.

**Q11.** Speed $=36\div3=12$ km/h. Time $=60\div12=\boxed{5\text{ hours}}$.

**Q12.** Son 3 parts $=15$. One part $=15\div3=5$. Father 7 parts $=7\times5=\boxed{35\text{ years}}$.

**Q13.** $18:12$, divide by HCF 6: $(18\div6):(12\div6)=\boxed{3:2}$.

**Q14.** Total parts $3+2=5$. One part $150\div5=30$. Shares $3\times30=\boxed{£90}$, $2\times30=\boxed{£60}$.

**Q15.** More workers take fewer days (inverse proportion). Worker-days $=4\times6=24$. For 6 workers: $24\div6=\boxed{4\text{ days}}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) adding instead of multiplying when finding a share; (ii) confusing which side of the ratio gets the larger share; (iii) forgetting to convert units (map scale cm→km); (iv) treating inverse proportion as direct. Revisit Foundation 3 if several of these appear.

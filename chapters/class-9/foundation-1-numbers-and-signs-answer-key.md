# Foundation 1: Answer Key (Parent Only)

This page contains the answers and full solution methods for the **Practice Questions**. Keep it away from the student until they have attempted the questions.

> **How to use:** Ask your child to show their working for each question. Mark correct with a tick. For any wrong answer, go back to the first step where the method slipped and explain it using the method below — do not just give the final answer.

## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** $-12+5$: different signs, subtract the smaller size from the larger and keep the sign of the larger. $12-5=7$, larger is negative $\rightarrow \boxed{-7}$.

**Q2.** $7-15$: start at $7$, move $15$ left $\rightarrow \boxed{-8}$.

**Q3.** $(-6)\times(-7)$: two negative signs give a positive result. $6\times7=\boxed{42}$.

**Q4.** $(-45)\div(+9)=-5$, one negative sign $\rightarrow \boxed{-5}$.

**Q5.** $18-4\times3$: multiply first, $4\times3=12$, then $18-12=\boxed{6}$.

**Q6.** $5+(12\div3)^2$: brackets $12\div3=4$, square $4^2=16$, add $5+16=\boxed{21}$.

**Q7.** $2\times(7+3)-8\div4$: brackets $2\times10=20$, division $8\div4=2$, subtract $20-2=\boxed{18}$.

**Q8.** $12\div3\times2$: equal priority, left to right, $12\div3=4$, $4\times2=\boxed{8}$.

**Q9.** $\lvert-11\rvert+\lvert3\rvert=11+3=\boxed{14}$.

**Q10.** In $8,24,305$: the $2$ is in the ten-thousands place, place value $20,000$; face value of $8$ is $8$.

**Q11.** Between two negatives, the one closer to zero is greater: $-12>-20$, so $\boxed{-12}$.

**Q12.** $\text{dividend}=\text{divisor}\times\text{quotient}+\text{remainder}=7\times5+3=38$, so $\boxed{38=7\times5+3}$.

**Q13.** $-4+9-7=5-7=\boxed{-2^\circ\text{C}}$.

**Q14.** Signed change $=$ selling $-$ cost $=95-120=\boxed{-\text{₹}25}$; loss $=\boxed{\text{₹}25}$.

**Q15.** Heights $+84$ and $-37$. Difference $=84-(-37)=84+37=\boxed{121\text{ m}}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) treating $-3^2$ as $9$ instead of $-9$; (ii) doing multiplication before division every time instead of left to right; (iii) writing $-5-3=-2$ instead of $-8$; (iv) thinking $-3^2=9$. Revisit Foundation 1 if several of these appear.

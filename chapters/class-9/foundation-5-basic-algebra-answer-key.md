# Foundation 5: Answer Key (Parent Only)

This page contains the answers and full solution methods for the **Practice Questions**. Keep it away from the student until they have attempted the questions.

> **How to use:** Ask your child to show their working for each question. Mark correct with a tick. For any wrong answer, go back to the first step where the method slipped and explain it using the method below — do not just give the final answer.

## Full solution methods (explain mistakes using these steps)

**Q1.** All like terms ($x$): $(3+5-2)x=\boxed{6x}$.

**Q2.** Distribute 4: $8x-12+5$, combine $\boxed{8x-7}$.

**Q3.** $3x+7=22 \Rightarrow 3x=15 \Rightarrow x=\boxed{5}$.

**Q4.** $\frac{x}{4}=9 \Rightarrow x=9\times4=\boxed{36}$.

**Q5.** Distribute: $2x-10+3x+3=(2x+3x)+(-10+3)=\boxed{5x-7}$.

**Q6.** Perimeter $=2((x+4)+x)=2(2x+4)=\boxed{4x+8\text{ cm}}$.

**Q7.** Pen cost $x-5$. Total $3x+2(x-5)=3x+2x-10=\boxed{5x-10\text{ rupees}}$.

**Q8.** Let number be $x$: $2x+7=25 \Rightarrow 2x=18 \Rightarrow x=\boxed{9}$.

**Q9.** $x+(x+3)=27 \Rightarrow 2x+3=27 \Rightarrow 2x=24 \Rightarrow x=\boxed{12}$.

**Q10.** $2(w+w+3)=30 \Rightarrow 4w+6=30 \Rightarrow 4w=24 \Rightarrow w=\boxed{6\text{ m}}$.

**Q11.** $6p=72 \Rightarrow p=\boxed{₹12}$.

**Q12.** $x+(x+20)+90=180 \Rightarrow 2x+110=180 \Rightarrow 2x=70 \Rightarrow x=\boxed{35^\circ}$.

**Q13.** $5(2a-3)-2(a+4)=10a-15-2a-8=\boxed{8a-23}$.

**Q14.** Group like terms: $(7m+2m)+(-3n-5n)=\boxed{9m-8n}$.

**Q15.** $x-8=15 \Rightarrow x=15+8=\boxed{23}$.

> **Common mistakes to watch for:** (i) forgetting to distribute to the second term in a bracket; (ii) writing $4(2x-3)=8x-3$ instead of $8x-12$; (iii) adding instead of subtracting when undoing operations; (iv) collecting unlike terms (e.g. $x$ and $x^2$). Revisit Foundation 5 if several of these appear.

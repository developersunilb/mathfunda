# Basic Equations: Application Questions

Application questions for **Basic Equations**. Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Number puzzle

A number is doubled and 7 is added, giving 25. Find the number.

## 2. Buying pens

Pens cost ₹$p$ each. Buying 6 pens costs ₹72. Find the cost of one pen.

## 3. Ages

Sarah is $x$ years old. Her brother is 3 years older. Their combined age is 27. Find Sarah's age.

## 4. Garden fence

A rectangular garden has width $w$ m and length $w+3$ m. The perimeter is 30 m. Find the width.

## 5. Triangle angles

Two angles of a triangle are $x$ and $x+20$, and the third is $90^\circ$. Find $x$.

## 6. Mobile plan

A mobile plan costs ₹$m$ per month plus a ₹150 activation fee. The total for the first month is ₹850. Find the monthly cost.

## 7. Sweets shared

A box of sweets is shared equally among 5 children, and each gets 8 sweets. How many sweets were in the box?

## 8. Triangle perimeter

A triangle has sides $x$, $x+3$ and $x+5$ cm. The perimeter is 36 cm. Find $x$.

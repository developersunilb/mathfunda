## 1. Area of a rectangle (algebraic)

**Question:** A rectangle has length $(x+5)$ m and width $(x-2)$ m. Write an expression for its area and simplify.

**Method:** Area $=\text{length}\times\text{width}=(x+5)(x-2)$. Expand.

**Step 1 — Expand.**

$$
(x+5)(x-2)=x^2-2x+5x-10
$$

**Step 2 — Combine like terms.**

$$
x^2+3x-10
$$

$$
\boxed{x^2+3x-10\text{ m}^2}
$$

## 2. Square garden

**Question:** A square garden has side $(2x+3)$ m. Write an expression for its area and simplify.

**Method:** Area $=\text{side}^2=(2x+3)^2$. Use $(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2$ with $a=2x$, $b=3$.

**Step 1 — Apply identity.**

$$
(2x)^2+2(2x)(3)+3^2=4x^2+12x+9
$$

$$
\boxed{4x^2+12x+9\text{ m}^2}
$$

## 3. Factorise a quadratic

**Question:** Factorise $x^2+5x+6$.

**Method:** Find two numbers with product 6 and sum 5: 2 and 3.

**Step 1 — Write factors.**

$$
x^2+5x+6=(x+2)(x+3)
$$

$$
\boxed{(x+2)(x+3)}
$$

Check: $(x+2)(x+3)=x^2+3x+2x+6=x^2+5x+6$. ✓

## 4. Difference of squares

**Question:** Factorise $16y^2-25$.

**Method:** Recognise $(4y)^2-5^2=(4y-5)(4y+5)$.

$$
16y^2-25=(4y)^2-5^2=(4y-5)(4y+5)
$$

$$
\boxed{(4y-5)(4y+5)}
$$

## 5. Simplify product

**Question:** Simplify $(3a-4)(3a+4)$.

**Method:** Difference of squares $(a-b)(a+b)=a^2-b^2$ with $a=3a$, $b=4$.

$$
(3a)^2-4^2=9a^2-16
$$

$$
\boxed{9a^2-16}
$$

## 6. Expand and simplify

**Question:** Expand and simplify $(x-7)(x+2)$.

**Method:** FOIL.

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
x^2+2x-7x-14
$$

**Step 2 — Combine.**

$$
x^2-5x-14
$$

$$
\boxed{x^2-5x-14}
$$

## 7. Square of a binomial

**Question:** Expand $(5t-3)^2$.

**Method:** $(a-b)^2=a^2-2ab+b^2$ with $a=5t$, $b=3$.

**Step 1 — Apply identity.**

$$
(5t)^2-2(5t)(3)+3^2=25t^2-30t+9
$$

$$
\boxed{25t^2-30t+9}
$$

## 8. Factorise completely

**Question:** Factorise $x^2-x-12$.

**Method:** Find two numbers with product -12 and sum -1: $-4$ and $+3$.

**Step 1 — Split and group.**

$$
x^2-x-12=x^2-4x+3x-12=x(x-4)+3(x-4)
$$

**Step 2 — Common bracket.**

$$
(x-4)(x+3)
$$

$$
\boxed{(x-4)(x+3)}
$$

# Linear Inequalities: Full Solutions

Full worked solutions for the **Application Questions**, including diagrams where helpful.

## 1. Solve

**Question:** Solve $x+5>12$.

**Method:** Subtract 5 from both sides; sign stays the same.

**Step 1 — Subtract 5.**

$$
x>12-5=7
$$

$$
\boxed{x>7}
$$

## 2. Solve (sign flip)

**Question:** Solve $-2x<10$.

**Method:** Divide by $-2$; the inequality sign flips.

**Step 1 — Divide by $-2$.**

$$
x>\frac{10}{-2}=-5
$$

$$
\boxed{x>-5}
$$

## 4. Solve

**Question:** Solve $5-2x\ge1$.

**Method:** Subtract 5, then divide by $-2$ (sign flips).

**Step 1 — Subtract 5.**

$$
-2x\ge1-5=-4
$$

**Step 2 — Divide by $-2$ and flip.**

$$
x\le\frac{-4}{-2}=2
$$

$$
\boxed{x\le2}
$$

## 7. Word problem

**Question:** A student needs at least 40 marks to pass. She already has 28. How many more $m$ does she need?

**Method:** "At least" means $\ge$. Total $28+m\ge40$.

**Step 1 — Write inequality.**

$$
28+m\ge40
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
m\ge40-28=12
$$

$$
\boxed{m\ge12}
$$

## 8. Word problem

**Question:** A lift can carry at most 600 kg. Each box weighs 25 kg. Find the maximum number of boxes $b$ the lift can carry.

**Method:** "At most" means $\le$. Total weight $25b\le600$.

**Step 1 — Write inequality.**

$$
25b\le600
$$

**Step 2 — Solve.**

$$
b\le\frac{600}{25}=24
$$

Since boxes are whole, the maximum is 24.

$$
\boxed{b\le24;\text{ maximum }24\text{ boxes}}
$$

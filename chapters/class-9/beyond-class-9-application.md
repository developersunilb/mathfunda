# Beyond Class 9: Application Questions

Application questions for **Beyond Class 9** (complex numbers and radians). Attempt each on paper before checking the answer key.

## 1. Add complex numbers

Simplify $(5+3i)+(2-7i)$.

## 2. Subtract complex numbers

Simplify $(6-4i)-(1+2i)$.

## 3. Multiply complex numbers

Simplify $(1+2i)(3+i)$.

## 4. Powers of $i$

Simplify $i^{17}$.

## 5. Powers of $i$

Simplify $i^{22}$.

## 6. Degrees to radians

Convert $45^\circ$ to radians.

## 7. Degrees to radians

Convert $150^\circ$ to radians.

## 8. Radians to degrees

Convert $\frac{\pi}{6}$ radians to degrees.

## 9. Radians to degrees

Convert $\frac{3\pi}{2}$ radians to degrees.

## 10. Complex number modulus (intro)

For $z=3+4i$, find $|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$.

## 11. Complex conjugate

Write the complex conjugate of $2-5i$.

## 12. Solve with complex numbers

Solve $x^2+4=0$ for $x$ (allowing complex solutions).

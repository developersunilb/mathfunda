# Foundation 6: Worked Examples

Worked examples for **Algebraic Operations**: expanding, factorising and identities.

## Example 1: Expand two binomials

**Question:** Expand $(x+3)(x-2)$.

**Method:** Multiply each term in the first bracket by each term in the second (FOIL).

**Step 1 — Multiply.**

$$
x\cdot x + x(-2) + 3\cdot x + 3(-2)=x^2-2x+3x-6
$$

**Step 2 — Combine like terms.**

$$
x^2+x-6
$$

$$
\boxed{x^2+x-6}
$$

## Example 2: Difference of two squares

**Question:** Expand $(a+b)(a-b)$.

**Method:** Identity $a^2-b^2=(a+b)(a-b)$.

$$
(a+b)(a-b)=a^2-ab+ab-b^2=a^2-b^2
$$

$$
\boxed{a^2-b^2}
$$

## Example 3: Factorise by splitting the middle term

**Question:** Factorise $x^2-7x+12$.

**Method:** Find two numbers with product 12 and sum -7: $-3$ and $-4$.

**Step 1 — Split the middle term.**

$$
x^2-7x+12=x^2-3x-4x+12
$$

**Step 2 — Group in pairs.**

$$
(x^2-3x)+(-4x+12)=x(x-3)-4(x-3)
$$

**Step 3 — Take out the common bracket.**

$$
(x-3)(x-4)
$$

$$
\boxed{(x-3)(x-4)}
$$

## Example 4: Square of a sum

**Question:** Expand $(2x+5)^2$.

**Method:** Identity $(a+b)^2=a^2+2ab+b^2$, with $a=2x$, $b=5$.

**Step 1 — Apply identity.**

$$
(2x)^2+2(2x)(5)+5^2=4x^2+20x+25
$$

$$
\boxed{4x^2+20x+25}
$$

## Example 5: Factorise a difference of squares

**Question:** Factorise $9x^2-16$.

**Method:** Recognise $(3x)^2-4^2=(3x-4)(3x+4)$.

$$
9x^2-16=(3x)^2-4^2=(3x-4)(3x+4)
$$

$$
\boxed{(3x-4)(3x+4)}
$$

# Foundation 2: Practice Questions

Attempt these **without looking at the answers**. Write your working on paper. After you finish, ask a parent to unlock the separate answer key to check your work and explain any mistakes.

## Questions

1. Find $\frac{2}{3}+\frac{3}{4}$.
2. Calculate $3.75\times0.4$.
3. Find $15\%$ of $240$.
4. Find $\frac{5}{6}\div\frac{2}{3}$.
5. Write $0.375$ as a fraction in simplest form.
6. Simplify $\frac{5}{8}+\frac{1}{6}$.
7. Calculate $0.6\times0.25$.
8. Find $40\%$ of $350$.
9. Write $0.6$ as a fraction in simplest form.
10. Find $\frac{3}{5}\div\frac{2}{7}$.
11. A recipe for 4 people uses $\frac{2}{3}$ kg of sugar. How much for 6 people?
12. An item costs $\text{₹}600$ and is discounted by $15\%$. Find the discount and sale price.
13. Divide $\text{₹}900$ in the ratio $2:3$.
14. Express $30$ minutes as a fraction of an hour in simplest form.
15. Calculate $0.375\times\frac{4}{3}+25\%\text{ of }80$.

**Do not write answers on this page.** Use a separate sheet of paper for your working, then check against the parent answer key.

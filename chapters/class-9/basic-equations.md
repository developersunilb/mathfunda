## 1. The Golden Rule of Equations

An equation is like a balanced seesaw. Whatever you do to one side, you must do to the other side.

When a term moves across the equals sign, it performs its **opposite operation** on the other side. This is called **transposition**.

| Operation involving $x$ | Move it across by | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Addition | Subtraction | $x+5=9\implies x=9-5$ |
| Subtraction | Addition | $x-3=4\implies x=4+3$ |
| Multiplication | Division | $2x=10\implies x=\frac{10}{2}$ |
| Division | Multiplication | $\frac{x}{3}=4\implies x=4\times3$ |
| Square | Square root | $x^2=25\implies x=\pm\sqrt{25}=\pm5$ |
| Square root | Square | $\sqrt{x}=3\implies x=3^2=9$ |

> **Remember:** Transposition is just a shortcut for doing the same operation on both sides of the equation.

<div class="formula-box">

**Why $x^2=25$ has two solutions:** Both $5^2=25$ and $(-5)^2=25$. So the equation gives $x=5$ **or** $x=-5$, written $x=\pm5$. Do not drop the negative solution unless the context rules it out (for example, a length cannot be negative).

</div>

### Worked examples

<div class="formula-box">

**Example 1:** Solve $x+7=12$.

Subtract $7$ from both sides:

$$
x=12-7=\boxed{5}
$$

</div>

<div class="formula-box">

**Example 2:** Solve $3x-4=11$.

Add $4$ to both sides:

$$
3x=11+4=15
$$

Divide both sides by $3$:

$$
x=\frac{15}{3}=\boxed{5}
$$

</div>

## 2. The Reciprocal Shortcut for Fractions

When the variable is multiplied by a fraction, move the fraction across the equals sign and flip it upside down. The flipped fraction is called the **reciprocal**.

<div class="formula-box">

**The rule:**

$$
\frac{a}{b}x=c\quad\implies\quad x=c\times\frac{b}{a}
$$

</div>

The negative sign stays with the fraction. For example, the reciprocal of $-\frac{4}{5}$ is $-\frac{5}{4}$.

### Example 1: Basic fraction

**Problem:** Solve $\frac{2}{3}x=8$.

**Step 1 — Flip the fraction and multiply:**

$$
x=8\times\frac{3}{2}
$$

**Step 2 — Simplify:**

$$
x=\frac{24}{2}=\boxed{12}
$$

### Example 2: Negative fraction

**Problem:** Solve $-\frac{4}{5}x=12$.

**Step 1 — Keep the negative sign with the fraction, then flip it:**

$$
x=12\times\left(-\frac{5}{4}\right)
$$

**Step 2 — Divide 12 by 4 first, then multiply by $-5$:**

$$
x=3\times(-5)=\boxed{-15}
$$

<div class="formula-box">

**Why this works:** A fraction multiplied by its reciprocal equals $1$.

$$
\frac{2}{3}\times\frac{3}{2}=1
$$

The fraction cancels, leaving $x$ alone on one side.

</div>

## 1. Mixing paint

**Question:** Pink paint is made by mixing red and white in the ratio 2:5. How much red and white paint are needed to make 350 ml of pink paint?

**Method:** Total parts $=2+5=7$. One part $=350\div7=50$ ml.

**Step 1 — One part.**

$$
350\div7=50\text{ ml}
$$

**Step 2 — Each colour.**

Red (2 parts): $2\times50=100$ ml. White (5 parts): $5\times50=250$ ml.

## 2. Sharing money

**Question:** ₹900 is shared between A, B and C in the ratio 2:3:4. How much does each person receive?

**Method:** Total parts $=2+3+4=9$. One part $=900\div9=100$.

**Step 1 — One part.**

$$
900\div9=100
$$

**Step 2 — Each share.**

A: $2\times100=₹200$. B: $3\times100=₹300$. C: $4\times100=₹400$.

$$
\boxed{A=₹200,\quad B=₹300,\quad C=₹400}
$$

Check: $200+300+400=900$. ✓

## 4. Map scale

**Question:** A map scale is 1:50,000. Two towns are 6 cm apart on the map. What is the actual distance in kilometres?

**Method:** 1 cm on map $=50,000$ cm in real life. $6\text{ cm}\rightarrow6\times50,000=300,000$ cm. Convert cm to km: $100,000$ cm $=1$ km.

**Step 1 — Real distance in cm.**

$$
6\times50{,}000=300{,}000\text{ cm}
$$

**Step 2 — Convert to km.**

$$
300{,}000\div100{,}000=3\text{ km}
$$

$$
\boxed{3\text{ km}}
$$

## 6. Speed and time

**Question:** A cyclist covers 36 km in 3 hours. How long to cover 60 km at the same speed?

**Method:** Speed $=36\div3=12$ km/h. Time for 60 km $=60\div12=5$ hours.

**Step 1 — Speed.**

$$
36\div3=12\text{ km/h}
$$

**Step 2 — Time.**

$$
60\div12=5\text{ hours}
$$

$$
\boxed{5\text{ hours}}
$$

## 7. Ratio of ages

**Question:** The ratio of ages of a father and son is 7:3. If the son is 15 years old, how old is the father?

**Method:** Son is 3 parts $=15$. One part $=15\div3=5$. Father is 7 parts.

**Step 1 — One part.**

$$
15\div3=5\text{ years}
$$

**Step 2 — Father's age.**

$$
7\times5=35\text{ years}
$$

$$
\boxed{35\text{ years old}}
$$

## 8. Comparing ratios

**Question:** A class has 18 boys and 12 girls. Write the ratio of boys to girls in simplest form.

**Method:** Ratio $=18:12$. Divide both by HCF 6.

**Step 1 — Simplify.**

$$
18:12=(18\div6):(12\div6)=3:2
$$

$$
\boxed{3:2}
$$

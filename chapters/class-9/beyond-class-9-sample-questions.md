## Questions

1. Simplify $(5+3i)+(2-7i)$.
2. Simplify $(6-4i)-(1+2i)$.
3. Simplify $(1+2i)(3+i)$.
4. Simplify $i^{17}$.
5. Simplify $i^{22}$.
6. Convert $45^\circ$ to radians.
7. Convert $150^\circ$ to radians.
8. Convert $\frac{\pi}{6}$ radians to degrees.
9. Convert $\frac{3\pi}{2}$ radians to degrees.
10. For $z=3+4i$, find $|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}$.
11. Write the complex conjugate of $2-5i$.
12. Solve $x^2+4=0$ for $x$ (allowing complex solutions).
13. Simplify $(3+2i)+(4-5i)$.
14. Simplify $(2+3i)(1-4i)$.
15. Simplify $i^{23}$.

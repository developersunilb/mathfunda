## Diagnostic Assessment

This is **not** an examination. It is a 25–30 minute check of the skills that Class 9 mathematics depends on. A wrong answer is useful: it shows exactly what to repair.

> **Instructions for the student:**
> - Attempt every question independently, without a calculator.
> - Show the working on paper.
> - If a question is unfamiliar, leave it and move on.
> - Do not worry about the score.

> **Instructions for parents:** Please do not correct answers while she is working. Mark the paper afterwards and discuss the first wrong step, not just the final answer.

## 1. The 20 Questions

**Numbers and operations**

1. Calculate $348\div12$.
2. Simplify $-8+13-6$.
3. Find $\frac{2}{3}+\frac{3}{4}$.
4. Calculate $3.75\times0.4$.
5. Find $15\%$ of $240$.
6. Divide $360$ in the ratio $2:3$.
7. Find the HCF and the LCM of $18$ and $24$.
8. Simplify $2^3\times2^2$.

**Algebra**

9. Simplify $3x+5x-2x$.
10. Solve $3x+7=22$.

**Geometry and measurement**

11. Two angles of a triangle are $48^\circ$ and $67^\circ$. Find the third angle.
12. Find the area of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.

**Mixed skills**

13. Simplify $(-3)\times(-4)+(-2)$.
14. Find $\frac{5}{6}\div\frac{2}{3}$.
15. Write $0.375$ as a fraction in its simplest form.
16. Expand and simplify $4(2x-3)+5$.
17. Solve $\frac{x}{4}=9$.
18. Round $4.678$ to two decimal places, and state the place value of the digit $7$.
19. Find the perimeter of a rectangle measuring $12.5\text{ cm}$ by $8\text{ cm}$.
20. In which quadrant does $(-3,4)$ lie? Find the midpoint of the segment joining $(2,4)$ and $(8,10)$.

### Worked reasoning for the key questions

**Q2:** Start at $-8$, move $13$ steps right to $5$, then $6$ steps left to $-1$.

$$
-8+13-6=5-6=-1
$$

**Q3:** Make the denominators equal using the LCM $12$:

$$
\frac{2}{3}+\frac{3}{4}=\frac{8}{12}+\frac{9}{12}=\frac{17}{12}
$$

**Q4:** Multiply $375\times4=1500$, then place two decimal digits:

$$
3.75\times0.4=1.500=1.5
$$

**Q6:** The ratio has $2+3=5$ equal parts. One part is $360\div5=72$, so the shares are $2\times72=144$ and $3\times72=216$.

**Q7:** $18=2\times3^2$ and $24=2^3\times3$. The HCF uses the lowest powers, $2\times3=6$. The LCM uses the highest powers, $2^3\times3^2=72$.

**Q13:** Multiplication first, then addition:

$$
(-3)\times(-4)+(-2)=12-2=10
$$

**Q14:** Dividing by $\frac{2}{3}$ is the same as multiplying by its reciprocal $\frac{3}{2}$:

$$
\frac{5}{6}\times\frac{3}{2}=\frac{15}{12}=\frac{5}{4}
$$

**Q18:** The third decimal digit is $8$, so round the hundredths digit up: $4.68$. In $4.678$ the $7$ is in the hundredths place, so its place value is $\frac{7}{100}=0.07$.

**Q20:** In $(-3,4)$ the $x$-coordinate is negative and the $y$-coordinate is positive, so the point lies in Quadrant II. The midpoint is

$$
\left(\frac{2+8}{2},\frac{4+10}{2}\right)=(5,7)
$$

## 3. Marking and Diagnosis

Give **1 mark per question**. Do not deduct marks for a correct method with a small arithmetic slip; instead, mark the method separately as a note.

| Score out of 20 | What it suggests | Next step |
|---|---|---|
| 16–20 | Foundations are largely secure. | Quick revision, then begin Chapter 1 of the study plan. |
| 11–15 | Some gaps are blocking new work. | Repair the weak rows in the table below, then continue. |
| 6–10 | Several foundation skills need rebuilding. | Spend Days 2–5 on the foundation lessons before Chapter 1. |
| 0–5 | Start gently and rebuild confidence. | Work through the foundation lessons slowly with a parent, one small idea at a time. |

> **Important:** Do not judge by the total alone. A student can score 16 and still have a serious fraction gap that will affect mensuration and algebra. Use the skill table.

## 5. After the Diagnostic

1. Write the numbers of the Red questions at the top of the mistakes notebook.
2. Work through the matching foundation lesson.
3. Retry those exact questions after two days, without looking at the key.
4. Then continue with the 45-day plan from Day 2.

Speed is not the goal yet. Accuracy and understanding come first; speed follows from repeated, correct practice.

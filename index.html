BEHIND THE PROJECT

A father’s journey, from panic to patience

I built MathsFunda because I was a desperate father trying to teach my own child mathematics — and watching every lesson end in tears, mine as much as theirs. The textbooks moved too fast, the language felt like a wall, and the fear of “not being good at maths” was setting in before Class 9 had even begun.

So I started over. I broke each idea into the smallest possible step, wrote it in plain language, and tested it one evening at a time. Slowly, the panic turned into questions, the questions into answers, and the answers into confidence. My child didn’t suddenly love maths — but they stopped fearing it. That is the standard I hold every page on this site to: if it doesn’t make a nervous learner feel a little more capable, it doesn’t belong here.

— A father who once stared at a worksheet and wanted to walk away
